Notch Dice Privacy Policy

Effective July 31, 2026.

Studio YONA (“we”, “us”) operates the Notch Dice macOS app (“Service”). This policy explains the information used to provide local play and authenticated online rooms. It is a service-specific reference draft and is not legal advice.

1. Operator and contact

Studio YONA · Representative and privacy contact: Nayoung Lee · Seoul, Republic of Korea · studioyona.official@gmail.com.

2. Information and purposes

  • Anonymous session: Supabase anonymous user ID and session tokens for authentication, room membership, and reconnection. Tokens are stored in the Mac Keychain and the service backend.
  • Player information: Nickname and player identifier to display participants, turns, scores, and rankings.
  • Room and game state: Room code and name, members, snapshots, scores, turns, actions, emotes, and timestamps to synchronize multiplayer games, recover sessions, and prevent invalid actions.
  • Local data: Display language, preferred nickname, recent match history, and app settings stored on the Mac to provide the app experience.
  • Support: Email address and information voluntarily included in a support request.

Notch Dice does not normally collect names, passwords, payment card numbers, contacts, location, camera or microphone data, advertising identifiers, analytics data, or targeted advertising data.

3. Retention and deletion

Local settings and history remain on the Mac until the user deletes them or removes the app. Anonymous sessions remain while needed for online play and reconnection. Room and game data are retained only as needed for operation and may be cleaned up after inactivity; the current release policy uses a 24-hour inactive-room cleanup threshold. Information required by law is retained for the applicable period and then deleted.

You can leave a room and remove local history in the app. To request deletion of online anonymous-session or room data, email studioyona.official@gmail.com. We will verify the request and process it where technically and legally possible.

4. Providers and international processing

We use Supabase for authentication, database, and realtime multiplayer infrastructure. Supabase may process the listed service data on infrastructure whose country or region depends on project configuration and provider policy. We do not sell personal information or provide it for advertising.

5. Rights and security

Subject to applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or withdrawal of consent by contacting us. We use HTTPS, Keychain session storage, authenticated room authorization, private realtime channels, and input/state validation as part of the service design. No security measure can eliminate every internet or third-party infrastructure risk.

6. Children, advertising, and automated decisions

The Service is not designed specifically for children under 14. It uses no cookies, advertising SDK, targeted advertising, or advertising identifier. Game AI is an opponent feature and does not make decisions about a person’s rights, eligibility, or access.

7. Changes and complaints

We may update this policy when the Service or applicable law changes. Important changes will be announced in the app or on the Studio YONA website. For privacy complaints or external help, contact the Korea Internet & Security Agency at 118 or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee at 1833-6972.
