Notch Dice Privacy Policy

Initial Effective Date: July 31, 2026 · Prior Revision: November 4, 2026 · Announced: November 16, 2026 · Effective Date: November 17, 2026

1. Overview

Studio YONA ("Company") publishes this Privacy Policy for the Notch Dice macOS application ("Service") in compliance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and applicable privacy laws. The Company operates no proprietary user account system, no Company game server, and no server-stored in-app feedback feature.

2. Categories and Purposes of Information Processed

CategoryPurposeItems & Processing Location
On-Device Local Data (Not Collected by Company) Local/AI gameplay, match/league records and settings, multiplayer verification secrets Match/league history and settings, Game Center player/match identifiers, received match states/scores/verification data, pre-reveal random number secrets and fleet placements, and local operational diagnostic logs (stored solely on the user's Mac and never transmitted to Company servers)
Optional Feature (Apple Game Center) Game Center sign-in check, friend invitations/participation, match list display, turn/result/verification sync Game Center player identifier and display name, friend list (accessed only when requested by the user and permitted under Apple's access conditions), opponent identifier, and match state/actions/scores/results/verification data (transmitted via Apple GameKit without collection onto Company servers)
Paid Purchases & Restore (Apple App Store) Paid app and In-App Purchase verification, entitlement activation, and purchase restoration Apple App Store (StoreKit) product identifiers and transaction validity states (payment card details are handled exclusively by Apple and never collected by the Company)
Customer Support Inquiries (Optional · User-Sent) Responding to inquiries, bug reports, suggestions, privacy rights requests, and disputes Sender email address, email display name, inquiry contents, and any app/macOS version info, masked diagnostic logs, or screenshots voluntarily attached by the user
  • The current app provides no contact picker or message-sending feature and does not access your full Contacts or Messages library. Legacy Message-match records saved by earlier versions are not automatically deleted by updates, though those matches cannot be opened in the current app.
  • Pre-reveal random number secrets and fleet placements remain on the originating Mac until disclosure is required during game verification, at which point only necessary RNG/attack proofs and end-of-match fleet verification data are shared with the opponent via Game Center.
  • The Company never requests or collects government ID numbers or sensitive personal data.

3. Retention and Processing Periods

CategoryRetention Period
On-device match/league records, settings, private match secrets, and legacy Message-match data Stored on the user's Mac until deleted via in-app deletion controls or macOS app data removal
Apple Game Center account/match/activity records and App Store payment records Governed by Apple's Game Center and App Store policies and account management settings (deleting local Mac data alone does not delete copies held by Apple or opponents)
Customer support emails and rights-request records Retained for 3 years after inquiry resolution in accordance with statutory consumer complaint and dispute record standards under Korean e-commerce law, then destroyed (any statutory contract/withdrawal/payment records actually held by the Company are retained separately for 5 years where required by law)

4. Destruction Procedures and Methods

  • Procedures: Personal data whose retention period has expired or purpose has been achieved is destroyed without delay; any legally required records are stored separately and destroyed upon statutory expiration.
  • Methods: Electronic records managed by the Company (including support mailboxes, trash folders, and separate archives) are permanently deleted using irreversible technical methods, and any paper printouts are shredded or incinerated.
  • On-device data may be removed through in-app deletion controls or macOS app-data management (moving only the `.app` bundle to Trash may not remove all container data).

5. Third-Party Provision

The Company does not sell or provide personal information to third parties for advertising or marketing, except with prior user consent or pursuant to lawful legal process. When a user chooses to play Game Center Multiplayer, match, turn, and verification data are shared with opponents through Apple under Apple Game Center & Privacy (local and AI gameplay remain available without Game Center).

6. External Infrastructure and International Transfers

The Company uses Google LLC's Gmail infrastructure to receive and store customer support emails, while optional Game Center Multiplayer and App Store purchases run on Apple services chosen by the user.

Users may decline external or international processing by not sending support emails or by not enabling Game Center (local and AI gameplay remain unaffected, though email support or online multiplayer will be unavailable).

7. Rights of Data Subjects, Legal Representatives, and Children

Users (and legal representatives) may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, or withdrawal of consent regarding support data held by the Company at any time via the Privacy Officer email below, and the Company responds within the statutory period after minimal verification (on-device data, Apple-held data, and opponent copies must be managed in their respective locations). The Service does not direct personal data collection primarily to children under 14, and any personal information of a child under 14 received without required legal representative consent is promptly deleted upon discovery.

8. Security Measures

  • Administrative measures: Least-privilege access controls and two-factor authentication on support mailboxes, separate retention of statutory records, and deletion of unnecessary inquiry data
  • Technical measures: Per-account/per-match local data separation, file-permission protection for Private Match Secrets (pre-reveal RNG and fleet data), exclusion of contacts/messages/match packets from local diagnostic logs, and encrypted transport

9. Cookies, Advertising, and Automated Decisions

The macOS app uses no cookies, advertising identifiers, targeted advertising, or third-party analytics/tracking SDKs. The Company makes no automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects on users; in-game AI operates locally on device solely as a game opponent.

EEA/EU users may exercise rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent via studioyona.official@gmail.com and may lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority.
