Detective's Recorder Privacy Policy

This policy applies only to the Detective Recorder app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Studio YONA operates the App on the basis that recording and transcription are processed on device, without analytics SDKs or automatic uploads to Company transcription servers.

Effective Date: May 24, 2026

Studio YONA ("Company", "we", "us") values the privacy of users of the Detective Recorder iPhone, iPad, and Mac application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we process and how we handle it.

1. Summary

  • Personal information automatically collected directly by the Company during App use: none.
  • Information users may provide directly: support inquiry content, diagnostics files attached by the user, and minimal information the user provides for App Store purchase or refund inquiries.
  • Information the Company may process if it introduces in-app purchases, subscriptions, or Paid Features in the future: App Store transaction verification information needed to confirm Paid Feature entitlements, restore purchases, or respond to payment errors.
  • Information processed on the user's device: recording files, recording metadata, transcription results, app settings, optional location information, and diagnostics files generated by the user.
  • Information not collected by the Company: account registration data, advertising identifiers, personalized advertising data, app usage records through third-party analytics SDKs, or recording files, transcription results, location information, and diagnostics logs automatically sent to Company servers.
  • Third-party disclosure: the Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties. If a user chooses sharing or file export, the selected recipient's policy may apply.
  • Processing by service provider and platform: Apple Inc. processes paid App purchases and refunds through the App Store, and Apple iCloud may process synced app data when the user uses iCloud sync.
  • Privacy contact and rights requests: studioyona.official@gmail.com

3. Personal Information We Process

The App does not require a Company account. The App does not automatically send recording files, location information, diagnostics logs, or app usage records to Company servers. The Company does not automatically collect personal information during App use.

App Store Purchase and Payment Information

The App is sold as a paid download through the Apple App Store. Apple processes App purchases, payment handling, refund requests, and purchase history management. The Company does not directly collect or store payment method information such as card numbers or bank account numbers.

If a user directly contacts the Company about a purchase or refund, the Company may review the user's email address, inquiry content, and App Store purchase or refund-related materials provided by the user only to handle that inquiry.

The Company may introduce in-app purchases, subscriptions, or Paid Features in the future. In that case, the Company may process purchased product identifiers, purchase status, subscription status, transaction identifiers, receipts, purchase verification tokens, or App Store transaction information to the extent necessary to confirm Paid Feature entitlements, restore purchases, or respond to payment errors. Actual payment method information is processed by Apple, and the Company does not directly collect or store it.

Information Processed on the Device

  • Recording files
  • Recording title, creation and modification time, duration, favorite status, deletion and recovery status
  • Transcription text, transcription progress status, and transcription error status generated by on-device transcription features
  • Categories, folders, sorting, storage quality, sharing format, transcription feature status, and other app settings
  • Recording start location when the user enables the optional location feature
  • Diagnostics files generated directly by the user

This information is stored by default on the user's device. Transcription is processed on device through Apple SpeechAnalyzer on iOS and the app-bundled Whisper-based path on Mac. The Company does not access or collect recording files or transcription results unless the user takes a separate action such as sharing, exporting, or attaching them to a support inquiry.

If the user uses iCloud sync, recording data, transcript text, and related metadata may be stored in the user's Apple iCloud account and processed by Apple to provide iCloud and CloudKit services. The Company does not receive or operate a separate copy of that synced data on Company servers.

Diagnostics Files Provided by the User

Diagnostics files are generated on the device only when the user chooses to export them from the App. If the user provides a diagnostics file to the Company through a support inquiry, the Company may review the following information only for support handling:

  • App error records, workflow records, and MetricKit diagnostics summaries
  • App version, build number, device model, and operating system version
  • Diagnostics information such as file paths shortened or masked by the App
  • Email address and inquiry content provided by the user to receive a response

4. Purposes of Processing

The Company processes personal information only for the following purposes:

  • Responding to customer inquiries and technical support requests
  • Providing guidance for App Store purchase or refund inquiries submitted directly by the user
  • Confirming entitlements, restoring purchases, and responding to payment errors for in-app purchases, subscriptions, or Paid Features that may be provided in the future
  • Processing user-selected recording files on device for transcription, showing transcription results, and supporting search and organization
  • Responding to support inquiries and disputes
  • Keeping transaction records and handling rights requests as required by applicable law

5. Retention Period

  • Support inquiry information: retained for the period necessary to respond to the inquiry and handle disputes.
  • Payment verification information related to in-app purchases, subscriptions, or Paid Features that may be provided in the future: retained for the period necessary to confirm entitlements, restore purchases, respond to support inquiries, and handle disputes.
  • Records on contracts, withdrawal of offers, payment, and supply of digital goods under Korean e-commerce law: 5 years.
  • Records on consumer complaints or dispute handling: 3 years.
  • Records on labels and advertising: 6 months.
  • Recording files, recording metadata, transcription results, app settings, location information, and diagnostics files on the device or in the user's Apple iCloud account when iCloud sync is used: retained until the user deletes them in the App, deletes App data, turns off or removes iCloud data according to Apple settings, or deletes the App data. The Company does not store them on a separate Company server.

6. Deletion

The Company deletes personal information without delay when the retention period expires or the processing purpose has been achieved.

  • Support inquiry information retained by the Company: deleted or de-identified in a manner that makes recovery difficult.
  • Payment verification information retained by the Company in the future: deleted or de-identified in a manner that makes recovery difficult.
  • Local app data: deleted through user deletion actions, app data deletion, or operating system app deletion.
  • Transcription results: deleted when the linked recording is deleted, transcription results are deleted, App data is deleted, or the operating system deletes the App.
  • iCloud-synced app data: deleted or retained according to the user's App deletion, iCloud, and Apple account settings.
  • Information that must be retained by law: stored separately for the statutory period and then deleted.

7. Third-Party Disclosure

The Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties, except in the following cases:

  • The user chooses recording file sharing, file export, or the system share sheet.
  • Disclosure is required by law or by a lawful request from a public authority.
  • The user separately consents.

Files sent by the user through sharing features are handled according to the policy of the selected app, person, or storage location.

10. Cookies, Tracking, Advertising, and Analytics

The App currently does not use web cookies, personalized advertising, advertising identifiers (IDFA), third-party tracking SDKs, analytics SDKs, or marketing SDKs. On-device transcription features do not automatically send recording files or transcription results to Company servers or external transcription servers. If the Company introduces any automatic collection mechanism, it will update this Privacy Policy and provide required notices in advance.

11. Automated Decisions

The App does not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on users. On-device transcription is an assistive feature that converts user-selected recordings into text and does not automatically determine users' rights, obligations, eligibility, access, or payment status. The Company does not use users' recording files or transcription results to train AI models.

13. Security Measures

The Company applies the following measures to protect app data and support inquiry information:

  • Minimizing app data access scope
  • Using operating system app sandboxing and file protection features
  • Processing recording files and transcription results on device by default and restricting external server transmission
  • Masking sensitive paths in diagnostics information
  • Restricting unnecessary server transmission
  • Minimizing access rights and retention periods for support inquiry information
  • Minimizing access rights and retention periods if payment verification information is processed in the future
  • Applying access control, encryption, log management, and permission management when retaining support materials
