Detective's Recorder Privacy Policy

This policy applies only to the Detective Recorder app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Studio YONA operates the App on the basis that recording and transcription are processed on device, without analytics SDKs or automatic uploads to Company transcription servers.

Effective Date: May 24, 2026

Studio YONA ("Company", "we", "us") values the privacy of users of the Detective Recorder iPhone, iPad, and Mac application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we process and how we handle it.

1. Summary

  • Personal information collected directly by the Company: payment verification information needed to confirm in-app purchase and subscription entitlements.
  • Information processed on the user's device: recording files, recording metadata, transcription results, app settings, optional location information, and diagnostics files generated by the user.
  • Information not collected by the Company: account registration data, advertising identifiers, personalized advertising data, app usage records through third-party analytics SDKs, or recording files, transcription results, location information, and diagnostics logs automatically sent to Company servers.
  • Third-party disclosure: the Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties. If a user chooses sharing or file export, the selected recipient's policy may apply.
  • Processing by service provider: Apple Inc. processes App Store in-app purchases and subscriptions. Transcription runs on device with the model included in the App and is not entrusted to a separate transcription server.
  • Privacy contact and rights requests: studioyona.official@gmail.com

3. Personal Information We Process

The App does not require a Company account. The App does not automatically send recording files, transcription results, location information, diagnostics logs, or app usage records to Company servers. The Company processes only the payment verification information needed to confirm in-app purchase and subscription entitlements.

Payment Verification Information Processed by the Company

  • Purchased product identifier
  • Purchase status, subscription status, and transaction identifier
  • Receipt, purchase verification token, or App Store transaction information needed to confirm paid feature entitlements
  • Minimal inquiry information needed for purchase restoration, refund support, subscription status checks, or payment error handling

Actual payment method information, such as card numbers or bank account numbers, is processed by Apple. The Company does not directly collect or store that information.

Information Processed on the Device

  • Recording files
  • Recording title, creation and modification time, duration, favorite status, deletion and recovery status
  • Transcription text, transcription progress state, and transcription error state generated by the on-device transcription feature
  • Categories, folders, sorting, storage quality, sharing format, transcription feature state, and other app settings
  • Recording start location when the user enables the optional location feature
  • Diagnostics files generated directly by the user

This information is stored by default on the user's device. Transcription is processed with the Whisper-based on-device model included in the App. The Company does not access or collect recording files or transcription results unless the user takes a separate action such as sharing, exporting, or attaching them to a support inquiry.

Diagnostics Files Provided by the User

Diagnostics files are generated on the device only when the user chooses to export them from the App. If the user provides a diagnostics file to the Company through a support inquiry, the Company may review the following information only for support handling:

  • App error records, workflow records, and MetricKit diagnostics summaries
  • App version, build number, device model, and operating system version
  • Diagnostics information such as file paths shortened or masked by the App

4. Purposes of Processing

The Company processes personal information only for the following purposes:

  • Confirming paid feature, subscription, and in-app purchase entitlements
  • Supporting purchase restoration, refunds, and subscription status checks related to App Store purchases
  • Checking suspected abuse or payment errors
  • Generating, displaying, searching, and organizing transcription results for recordings selected by the user on device
  • Responding to support inquiries and disputes
  • Keeping transaction records and handling rights requests as required by applicable law

5. Retention Period

  • Payment verification information: retained for the period necessary to confirm purchase status, restore purchases, respond to support inquiries, and handle disputes.
  • Records on contracts, withdrawal of offers, payment, and supply of digital goods under Korean e-commerce law: 5 years.
  • Records on consumer complaints or dispute handling: 3 years.
  • Records on labels and advertising: 6 months.
  • Recording files, recording metadata, transcription results, app settings, location information, and diagnostics files on the device: retained on the device until the user deletes them in the App or deletes the App data. The Company does not store them on a separate Company server.

6. Deletion

The Company deletes personal information without delay when the retention period expires or the processing purpose has been achieved.

  • Payment verification information retained by the Company: deleted or de-identified in a manner that makes recovery difficult.
  • Local app data, including transcription results: deleted through user deletion actions, app data deletion, or operating system app deletion.
  • Information that must be retained by law: stored separately for the statutory period and then deleted.

7. Third-Party Disclosure

The Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties, except in the following cases:

  • The user chooses recording file sharing, file export, or the system share sheet.
  • Disclosure is required by law or by a lawful request from a public authority.
  • The user separately consents.

Files sent by the user through sharing features are handled according to the policy of the selected app, person, or storage location.

10. Cookies, Tracking, Advertising, and Analytics

The App currently does not use web cookies, personalized advertising, advertising identifiers (IDFA), third-party tracking SDKs, analytics SDKs, or marketing SDKs. The on-device transcription model is included in the App, and the App does not automatically send recording files or transcription results to Company servers or external transcription servers for transcription. If the Company introduces any automatic collection mechanism beyond payment verification, it will update this Privacy Policy and provide required notices in advance.

11. Automated Decisions

The App does not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on users. The on-device transcription feature is an assistive feature that converts recordings selected by the user into text. It does not automatically determine user rights, obligations, eligibility, access, or payment status. The Company does not use users' recording files or transcription results to train AI models.

12. User Rights

Users may exercise the following rights regarding their personal information:

  • Access
  • Correction
  • Deletion
  • Suspension of processing
  • Withdrawal of consent
  • Data portability requests, subject to the applicable legal scope and technical availability for the service

Users may submit requests through in-app features or by emailing studioyona.official@gmail.com. The Company will handle requests within the period required by applicable law.

The App is not directed to children under 14. If the Company learns that it processes personal information of a child under 14, the legal representative may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing through the contact above.

13. Security Measures

The Company applies the following measures to protect app data and payment verification information:

  • Minimizing app data access scope
  • Using operating system app sandboxing and file protection features
  • Masking sensitive paths in diagnostics information
  • Processing recording files and transcription results primarily on device and restricting unnecessary server transmission
  • Minimizing access rights and retention periods for payment verification information
  • Applying access control, encryption, log management, and permission management for payment verification processing
