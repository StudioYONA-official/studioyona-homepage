Detective's Recorder Privacy Policy

This draft applies only to Detective's Recorder. Studio YONA is keeping the MVP intentionally minimal: no analytics on the website, email-only support, and app-scoped legal pages for App Store review.

Effective date: May 24, 2026

Studio YONA (the "Company") publishes this Privacy Policy for users of the Detective's Recorder mobile application (the "App") under applicable privacy laws.

1. Summary

  • Personal information directly collected by the Company: payment verification information needed to confirm in-app purchase and subscription entitlements
  • Information processed on device: recording files, recording metadata, app settings, optional location information, and diagnostic files the user directly creates
  • Information not collected: Studio YONA account information, advertising identifiers, personalized advertising data, app usage records through third-party analytics SDKs, and recording files, location information, or diagnostic logs automatically sent to Company servers
  • Third-party provision: the Company does not provide personal information to third parties. If the user chooses sharing features or iCloud sync, information may be processed under the selected destination or Apple service policies.
  • Processing entrustment: Apple Inc. processes in-app purchases and subscription payments.
  • Contact and rights requests: studioyona.official@gmail.com

3. Personal Information Processed

The App does not require Studio YONA account registration and does not automatically send recording files, location information, diagnostic logs, or app usage records to Company servers. The Company processes only payment verification information needed to confirm in-app purchase and subscription entitlements.

Payment verification information processed by the Company

  • Purchased product identifier
  • Purchase status, subscription status, transaction identifier
  • Receipt, purchase verification token, or App Store transaction information needed to confirm paid feature entitlement
  • Minimum inquiry information needed for purchase restoration, refunds, subscription status checks, and payment error support

Actual card numbers, account numbers, and other payment method details are processed by Apple. The Company does not directly collect or store them.

Information processed on device

  • Recording files
  • Recording title, creation and modification time, playback length, favorite status, deletion and recovery status
  • Categories, folders, sorting, language, storage quality, sharing format, and other app settings
  • Recording start location if the optional feature is enabled
  • Diagnostic files directly created by the user

This information is stored on the user's device by default. The Company does not access or collect it unless the user separately shares, exports, attaches it to an inquiry, or enables iCloud sync.

When the user directly provides diagnostic files

Diagnostic files are created on device when the user directly exports them in the App. If the user directly provides a diagnostic file through support, the Company may review the following information to handle the inquiry.

  • App error records, workflow records, MetricKit diagnostic summaries
  • App version, build number, device model, operating system version
  • Diagnostic information such as file paths shortened or masked by the App

4. Purposes of Processing

  • Confirming paid feature, subscription, and in-app purchase entitlements
  • Supporting purchase restoration, refunds, subscription status checks, and other App Store purchase issues
  • Checking abuse or payment errors
  • Responding to support requests and disputes
  • Preserving transaction records and processing rights requests under applicable law

5. Retention Period

  • Payment verification information: retained for the period necessary to confirm purchase status, restore purchases, respond to inquiries, and handle disputes
  • Records on contracts, withdrawal, payment, and supply under Korean e-commerce consumer protection laws: 5 years
  • Consumer complaint or dispute records: 3 years
  • Display and advertising records: 6 months
  • On-device recording files, metadata, app settings, location information, and diagnostic files: stored on device until the user deletes them in the App or deletes app data. The Company does not store them on a separate server.
  • iCloud sync data: governed by the user's Apple iCloud account settings and Apple policies

6. Destruction

  • Payment verification information held by the Company: deleted or de-identified in a way that is difficult to restore
  • Local app data: deleted through the user's deletion action, app data deletion, or operating system app deletion process
  • iCloud sync data: governed by the user's iCloud settings, app deletion, sync data deletion process, and Apple policies
  • Records required by law: separately retained for the required period and then destroyed

7. Third-Party Provision

The Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties, except in the following cases.

  • When the user directly chooses to share recording files, export files, or use the system share sheet
  • When the user enables iCloud sync and synchronization occurs between user devices through Apple iCloud
  • When there is a legal basis or a lawful request from an investigative or public authority
  • When the user separately consents

Files sent through sharing features may be processed under the policies of the selected app, person, or storage destination.

10. Cookies and Behavioral Information

The App currently does not use web cookies, personalized advertising, IDFA, third-party tracking SDKs, analytics SDKs, or marketing SDKs. If the Company introduces automatic collection tools other than payment verification, it will update this Privacy Policy and provide required notices in advance.

11. Automated Decisions

The App currently does not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on users.

12. User and Legal Representative Rights

Users may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, withdrawal of consent, and data transfer. The legal applicability and technical scope of data transfer requests may vary depending on service scale and delivery method.

Rights requests may be made through App features or studioyona.official@gmail.com, and the Company will process requests within the period required by law.

The Company does not target children under 14 as primary users. If the Company becomes aware that it processes personal information of a child under 14, the legal representative may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing through the contact above.

13. Security Measures

  • Minimizing app data access scope
  • Using operating system app sandboxing and file protection features
  • Masking sensitive paths in diagnostic information
  • Restricting unnecessary server transmission
  • Minimizing access permissions and retention periods for payment verification information
  • Applying access control, encryption, log management, and permission management for payment verification processing
