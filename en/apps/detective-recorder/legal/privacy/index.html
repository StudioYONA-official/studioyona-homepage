Detective's Recorder Privacy Policy

Initial Effective Date: May 24, 2026 · Announced: November 16, 2026 · Effective Date: December 17, 2026

Studio YONA ("Company", "we", "us") values the privacy of users of the Detective's Recorder iPhone, iPad, and Mac application ("App") and publishes this Privacy Policy in accordance with applicable privacy laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea.

1. Summary

  • Personal information automatically collected directly by the Company during App use: none.
  • Information users may provide directly: customer support email address and message content, diagnostics files voluntarily attached by the user, and minimal proof submitted for App Store purchase or refund inquiries.
  • Information processed on the user's device (and private iCloud if enabled): recording files, recording metadata, on-device transcription results, app settings, optional location tags, and user-generated diagnostics files.
  • Information not collected by the Company: proprietary account data, advertising identifiers (IDFA), personalized advertising data, third-party analytics SDK records, or automatic uploads of recordings, transcripts, locations, or logs to Company servers.
  • External platforms and mail handling: the Company engages no ongoing server data processors; Apple Inc. handles paid App billing/refunds and optional private iCloud sync, while Google LLC (Gmail) handles customer support emails.
  • Privacy contact and rights requests: studioyona.official@gmail.com

3. Personal Information Processed and Purposes

3-1. App Store Paid Download Information

The App is sold solely as a paid download through the Apple App Store. Billing, receipts, and refund execution are handled by Apple under Apple's policies, and the Company never directly collects or stores payment card or bank account details. Only when a user emails the Company regarding a purchase or refund does the Company review the user's email address, message, and submitted proof to assist with the inquiry.

3-2. Information Processed on Device and in Private iCloud (Not Collected by Company)

  • Items: recording files, titles, timestamps, duration, favorites, Recently Deleted status, on-device transcript text and status, category/quality settings, and optional recording-start location tags.
  • Purpose & Path: recording, segment trimming, search, organization, and cross-device sync. Speech-to-text transcription runs locally on the device using the operating system's speech engine or a bundled on-device recognition model without transmitting audio or text to Company servers. When iCloud sync is enabled by the user, data is stored solely in the user's private Apple iCloud (CloudKit) container.

3-3. Customer Support Inquiries and User-Exported Diagnostics

  • Items (optional): sender email address, inquiry content, and any diagnostics file voluntarily exported and attached by the user (error/workflow summaries, OS crash/performance diagnostics summaries, App/OS version, device model, and masked file paths).
  • Purpose: responding to customer inquiries and technical support, troubleshooting bugs, handling privacy rights requests, and resolving disputes.

4. Retention Period and Destruction

  • Customer support inquiries and attachments: Retained for 3 years after inquiry resolution in accordance with statutory consumer complaint and dispute record standards under Korean e-commerce law, then promptly destroyed (or separately retained for 5 years where statutory contract/payment record retention applies).
  • On-device and private iCloud data: Retained under user control until deleted in the App, via App deletion, or through the user's Apple iCloud settings.
  • Destruction methods: Electronic files are irreversibly deleted using technical methods, and any paper printouts are shredded or incinerated.

5. Third-Party Disclosure

The Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties, except where the user initiates sharing/export through the OS share sheet (governed by the recipient's policy), separately consents, or where required by a lawful request from a public authority under applicable law.

6. External Platforms and International Processing

The Company does not upload in-app recordings or transcripts to its own servers. However, paid App downloads, optional iCloud sync, and email support involve international platform infrastructure as follows:

7. Cookies, Tracking, and Automated Decisions

The App uses no web cookies, personalized advertising, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or third-party tracking/analytics SDKs. The App makes no automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects on users, and the Company never uses user recordings or transcripts to train AI models.

8. Rights of Data Subjects and Legal Representatives

Users (and legal representatives) may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, or withdrawal of consent regarding support records held by the Company via studioyona.official@gmail.com, and the Company takes action within the statutory period. The App does not direct account registration or personal data collection primarily to children under 14, and any personal information of a child under 14 submitted without legal representative consent is promptly deleted upon discovery.

9. Security Measures

  • On-device recording and transcription architecture with OS app sandboxing and file protection
  • Masking of sensitive paths in user-exported diagnostic files
  • Least-privilege access controls, two-factor authentication, and encrypted transport for support mailboxes
