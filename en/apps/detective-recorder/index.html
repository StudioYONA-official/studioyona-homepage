Detective's Recorder?

Ever dug around for a recording?

Once files pile up, it is hard to find the one you need among near-identical titles. So we made the app decide where a recording belongs before it starts. Tap a category and recording begins; by the time you stop, it is already filed.

  • One tapTap a category and recording starts right there
  • On-deviceTranscription runs on the device and is not sent anywhere
  • Two levelsGroups and categories keep things findable as the list grows
How it works

Here is how you use it

The usual order, shown with the actual app screens.

  1. Create groups and categories
  2. Tap to record
  3. Read it back
  4. Kept on device
01

Create groups and categories

Set up the subjects you record often, like lectures, meetings, or interviews. Bundling them into groups keeps things findable.

02

Tap and it records

Recording starts the moment you tap a category. You do not pick a save location or invent a name, so you miss fewer moments.

03

Read it back as text

Read the transcript and find the part you need. No need to replay from the beginning.

04

It stays on your device

Audio and transcripts are stored on the device and not sent to an external transcription service. If you delete something by mistake, you can restore it from Recently Deleted.

Key features

Easy to find, even with a lot

The more you record, the more organization matters. We added search, favorites, batch actions, and restore.

  • Search and favorites

    You can search the library and keep the recordings you play often in one place.

  • Editing and batch actions

    Trim sections or continue a recording, and move or share several at once.

  • Widgets

    Start a recording straight from the home screen or even the lock screen.

Where it fits

When it comes in handy

  • Lectures and classes

    One category per course collects a whole semester in one place. Come exam season, pull up just the session you need.

  • Meetings and interviews

    Group them by project, then read the transcript afterward to find the part you need.

  • Field notes

    For site visits and inspections where you may need evidence later. Turn on location tagging and the place is recorded too.

  • Thinking out loud

    Leave an idea as speech. With silence auto-stop on, it ends on its own when you stop talking.
