How Detective's Recorder works

Filed the moment you press record, transcribed without external servers

When you hit record first and promise yourself you will sort files later, you end up with hundreds of dated filenames in one flat list. Detective's Recorder organizes subjects into two levels—Groups and Categories—so tapping a subject starts recording straight into that folder with automatic numbering, then transcribes audio locally on your device.

  • 1-tap filingTap a category to start recording with automatic category numbering
  • Local speech-to-textUses on-device speech recognition on iPhone/iPad and local Whisper on Mac
  • Segment recoveryCheckpoints audio every 5–30 seconds during recording to prevent data loss
How it works

Tap a subject to record, then scan the transcript

Never miss the opening seconds of a conversation while hunting for a folder or typing a title.

  1. Set up groups & categories
  2. Tap a topic to record
  3. Transcribe locally
  4. Edit, share & sync via iCloud
01

Organize subjects into groups and categories

Set up recurring subjects—courses, client meetings, interviews, or field inspections—in a clean two-level tree (Group > Category).

02

Tap a category to start recording right away

Launch from the app, Lock Screen or Home Screen widgets, or app icon quick actions. Audio is checkpointed in segments while recording, so even an unexpected shutdown can be recovered on next launch.

03

Transcribe right after recording or while charging

Convert recordings to text automatically when you stop, or queue long recordings to transcribe only while your device is plugged into power.

04

Kept on your device and personal iCloud

Audio and transcripts never touch third-party transcription servers. Keep files on device or sync across iPhone, iPad, and Mac via your personal iCloud account, with customizable Recently Deleted retention.

Key features

Built for long sessions and growing archives

Combines reliable field capture with practical editing and organization tools.

  • Lock Screen & Home Screen widgets

    Jump straight into recording for a favorite category from the Lock Screen, Home Screen widgets, or app icon quick actions.

  • Append recording, trimming & audio quality options

    Continue recording onto the end of an existing file, trim unwanted sections, and choose between High Efficiency (AAC), Standard, or Apple Lossless quality.

  • Silence auto-stop, starting address & batch move

    Stop recording automatically after 10 seconds to 10 minutes of silence, optionally stamp the starting street address, and move or share multiple files at once.

Good for

Where it fits best

  • Confidential meetings, interviews & consultations

    Transcribe sensitive work discussions, interviews, or counseling sessions locally without uploading audio to external cloud AI services.

  • University lectures & seminars

    Keep a full semester of recordings neatly separated by course and review the transcripts before exams.

  • Long sessions & multi-part recordings

    Append to an existing recording after a break, and rely on 5–30 second segment checkpoints to protect multi-hour sessions.

    Start from a Lock Screen widget with silence auto-stop enabled so the recording saves automatically after you finish speaking.
