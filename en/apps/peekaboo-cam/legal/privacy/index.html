2. Information Collected by the Company

The Company directly collects or accesses personal information only when a user contacts support or voluntarily provides purchase-related materials.

3. On-Device Processing and Permissions

The following items are processed on the user's device to provide the Service and are not automatically sent to Company servers.

  • Camera preview, captured photos/videos, and camera settings such as focus, exposure, zoom, aspect ratio, timer, and flash
  • Microphone input during video recording and parent voice presets recorded by the user
  • Sound playback settings, repeat count, random/fixed playback settings, and front-camera visual cue settings
  • Captured media saved to the Photos library and app settings stored on the device

Attention sounds and visual cues are not composited into saved media. Users can manage local data through the Photos app, in-app delete controls, device settings, or app deletion.

4. Third-Party Disclosure

The Company does not sell captured media, videos, or voice presets and does not provide them to third parties without user consent. Exceptions are user-selected sharing to external apps or services, Apple's processing of App Store purchase/refund/In-App Purchase information under Apple's policies, and lawful requests under applicable law.

5. Processors and International Transfers

The current Service does not continuously outsource personal information to Company-controlled external servers. Support email is processed only when a user sends an inquiry. App Store purchase, refund, download, and In-App Purchase information is processed by Apple Inc. and its affiliates in countries where Apple operates its services under Apple's policies.

If service features or personal information processing practices change, the Company will update this Policy and the app or App Store information with the actual processors, processing items, and transfer details.

6. Deletion and User Rights

The Company deletes personal information without delay when the processing purpose is achieved or the retention period expires. Users exercise access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, withdrawal of consent, and complaint handling rights by contacting studioyona.official@gmail.com.

The Service is designed as a tool used by guardians and does not provide account-based services directed to children under 14.

7. Security, Tracking, and Automated Decisions

Captured media and voice presets are primarily handled on the user's device and Photos library. The Service does not use personalized ads, ad tracking, third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, cookies, behavioral advertising, or automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects for users.
