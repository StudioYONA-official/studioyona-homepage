3. On-Device Processing and Permissions (Not Collected by Company)

The following items are processed strictly on the user's device to provide the Service and are never sent to Company servers:

  • Camera preview, captured photos/videos, and camera settings such as focus, exposure, zoom, aspect ratio, timer, and flash
  • Microphone input during video recording and user-recorded voice presets
  • Sound playback settings, repeat count, random/fixed playback settings, and front-camera visual cue settings
  • Captured media saved to the Photos library and app settings stored on the device

Attention sounds and visual cues are not digitally composited into saved media. Users can manage local data directly through the Photos app, in-app delete controls, device settings, or app deletion.

4. Third-Party Disclosure

The Company does not sell captured media, videos, voice presets, or support records and does not provide them to third parties without user consent, except where the user selects external sharing, where Apple processes App Store purchase/refund records under Apple's policies, or where required by a lawful request under applicable law.

6. Destruction Procedures and Methods

When the processing purpose is fulfilled or the retention period expires, electronic files are irreversibly deleted using technical methods and any paper records are shredded or incinerated. Where retention is required by law, records are stored separately from other data for the statutory period and then destroyed. Files shared by the user to external apps or services cannot be deleted by the Company and must be removed under the recipient service's procedures.

7. Rights of Data Subjects and Legal Representatives

Users (and legal representatives) may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, or withdrawal of consent regarding support records held by the Company via studioyona.official@gmail.com, and the Company will respond promptly in accordance with applicable law.

The Service is designed as a shooting aid for guardians and does not direct account registration or personal data collection primarily to children under 14. Any personal information of a child under 14 submitted without legal representative consent is promptly deleted upon discovery.

8. Security Measures

  1. Captured media and voice presets are processed exclusively on the user's device and Photos library.
  2. No personalized ads, ad tracking, or third-party advertising/analytics SDKs are used.
  3. Device permissions (Camera, Microphone, Photos Add) are requested strictly within the minimum scope required for features.
  4. Support mailbox access is minimized with two-factor authentication, and payment credentials are never stored by the Company.

9. Cookies, Tracking, and Automated Decisions

The Company uses no cookies, advertising-identifier tracking, or behavioral marketing, performs no automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects on users, and never uses user media or voice presets to train AI models.
