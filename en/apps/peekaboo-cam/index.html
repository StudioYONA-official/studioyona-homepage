How Peekaboo Cam works

Guides their eyes to the top lens instead of the middle of the screen

In front-camera selfies, kids naturally stare at their own face in the center of the screen, making their eyes look downward in every photo. And generic toy sounds lose a child or pet's interest after two or three tries. Peekaboo Cam combines five custom family voice recordings with animated visual icons right next to the front lens.

  • 5 family voicesRecord nicknames or favorite words and play them in photo or video mode
  • 5-level lens cuesHigh-contrast B&W or color icons that boost right before capture & on timer's last second
  • 100% clean mediaVisual cues never appear in your saved photos or videos
How it works

Record once, toggle cues right from the viewfinder

Turn sound and visual cues on or off from the top bar without leaving the camera screen.

  1. Set attention & camera options
  2. Record 5 voices & pick playback mode
  3. Choose lens-side icon & motion
  4. Save clean photos & videos
01

Configure attention cues and camera controls

Manage sound and visual cues alongside grid lines, front-camera mirroring, video stabilization, automatic macro switching, and volume-button shutter.

02

Record up to five voices they recognize

Save up to five voice clips—mom or dad's voice, a child's nickname, or a pet's favorite treat word—and play them in Fixed, smart Random (avoids immediate repeats), or Repeat mode.

03

Customize the animated icon beside the front lens

Pick from five icon shapes (arrows, smiley, stripes, concentric rings, checkerboard), High-Contrast B&W or Color palettes, and five stackable motions across Levels 1–5—with an automatic boost right before capture and on the last second of a 3s/10s timer.

04

Saved photos and videos stay completely clean

On-screen visual cues are never baked into your saved media. Everything saves straight to your device's Photos library with no account or external server.

Key features

Native camera optics paired with attention cues

Keeps the optical zoom, macro, and focus controls of a full camera while handling the calling for you.

  • Voice playback during video & QuickTake hold-to-record

    Press and hold the photo shutter to record video immediately without switching modes, and trigger recorded family voices while recording to capture genuine reactions.

  • High-contrast B&W mode for infants & 5 stackable motions

    Switch between High-Contrast Black & White for young babies and vibrant Color mode, and combine shake, blink, pulse, clone, and spin animations across five intensity levels.

  • 0.5x–3x optical lenses, auto macro & AE/AF lock

    Supports one-tap 0.5x, 1x, 2x, and 3x optical lens switching, pinch zoom up to 10x, automatic ultra-wide macro switching, long-press AE/AF lock with an exposure slider, and 4:3, 1:1, and 16:9 ratios.
