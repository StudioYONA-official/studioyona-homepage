Peekaboo Cam

A guardian camera app that helps children look toward the camera during photos and videos. Parent voice presets, basic sounds, and front-camera visual cues make capture moments easier to prepare.

Key features

  • Photo and video capture with front/rear camera switching
  • Up to five parent voice presets and basic sound playback
  • Front-camera visual cues positioned near the lens area
  • Timer, zoom, aspect ratio, focus/exposure lock, and stabilization

App info

  • Category: Photo & Video
  • Compatibility: iOS
  • Languages: Korean, English
  • Price: Planned as a paid app

Clean saved media

Attention sounds and visual cues are not composited into saved media. Only the captured photos and videos are saved to the device's Photos library.
