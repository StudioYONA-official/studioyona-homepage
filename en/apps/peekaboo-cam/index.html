Peekaboo Cam?

They will not look at the camera

Holding the phone in one hand while trying to catch their eye with the other leaves nothing to press the shutter with. A voice you recorded earlier and a light beside the lens draw the attention of a child or a pet, so you can focus on the shot.

  • 5Record the voices a child or pet actually reacts to
  • 5 levelsTune the light beside the lens to suit a child or pet

How it works

Here is how you use it

The usual order, shown with the actual app screens.

  1. Set up the cues
  2. Record a voice
  3. Adjust the light
  4. Not in the result
01

Set up attention cues and capture

You can set the sound and light cues from one screen. Capture options like automatic macro and volume-button shutter are here too.

02

Record the voice you call with

The phrase that gets a reaction differs in every home. Record up to five and choose fixed or random playback.

03

Adjust the light beside the lens

Set the type, motion, and intensity to suit a child or pet. It brightens automatically right before capture and on a timer's final second.

04

None of it reaches the photo

Sounds and lights appear on screen only and are never composited into saved media. Only the child or pet is in the photo.

Key features

Let the app do the calling

Making noise and pressing the shutter at the same time is hard. So we made the app handle the calling for you.

  • Voices and built-in sounds

    Play five presets you recorded yourself plus the bundled sounds, in fixed, random, or repeat mode.

  • The controls you need

    Supports 3s and 10s timers, pinch zoom, aspect ratio selection, tap focus, and AE/AF lock.

  • Keeps going during video

    Sounds still play while recording, so you can catch the reaction as it happens.

Where it fits

When it comes in handy

  • Shooting alone

    You do not need a second person to call them. The app makes the noise and you handle the shutter.

  • Milestone photos

    For sessions where they have to look up at a specific moment. The light brightens on the timer's last second.

  • Shy children

    A child who freezes at the sight of a camera loosens up at a familiar voice.

  • Pets

    The same approach draws an animal's attention toward the lens.
