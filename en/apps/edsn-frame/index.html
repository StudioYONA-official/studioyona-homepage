01

What is EDSN Frame?

EDSN Frame turns your phone into a photobooth. Choose a cut layout and frame, tap Shoot, then refine the result with frames, text, stamps, and editing tools before saving.

  • You can shoot new photos or import existing photos and videos into the same photobooth-style result flow.
  • It works for personal use and for event-mode setups where guests shoot one after another at weddings, festivals, stores, or pop-ups.
02

Which frames can I shoot with?

Before shooting, choose between regular frames and special frames. Regular frames are for familiar photobooth layouts such as 1-cut, 2-cut, 4-cut, 6-cut, and 9-cut results.

  • If you are new, start with a simple vertical 4-cut or vertical 6-cut layout.
  • Special frames may include shooting styles such as Color Hunt, Reel Story, or Instant Photo.
  • Available frame types can vary by app version, owned frame packs, and shop purchase status.
03

What frame designs are available?

On the result screen, the Frame tab can include solid, gradient, and graphic frame designs. Solid frames are clean, gradients add soft color, and graphic frames are better for themed or event-style results.

  • The shop shows which frame packs you own and which ones are still locked.
  • Locked frames may open through points, cash purchases, subscriptions, or items that are still being prepared.
04

How do I use filters or correction tools?

After shooting or importing, open the Edit tab on the result screen. Use it for adjustments such as brightness, contrast, saturation, color temperature, or any filter options available in your app version.

  • Some editing tools or filter products may be marked as coming soon or may require a separate entitlement.
  • Before moving to the save step, check that faces, text, and stamps are not covered by the frame.
05

Can I use it for group events?

Yes. Event mode is designed for situations where many people use the same device in sequence, such as weddings, festivals, stores, pop-ups, or promotions. The operator prepares the frame and flow, then guests follow the screen and receive the result by QR or print.

Prepare before the event

  • Prepare a shooting device with EDSN Frame installed, a charger, power bank, and stand or tripod.
  • Use stable Wi-Fi or a hotspot if QR delivery will be used.
  • If printing is part of the event, check the printer, paper, ink or cartridge, and connection before guests arrive.
  • Always run one full test shoot as a staff member before the event starts.
06

What can I set in event mode?

In event mode, the operator prepares the flow guests will see. You typically check the frame, cut count, countdown, bottom copy, whether result editing is allowed, auto return, and whether delivery uses QR or a printer.

  • A 4-digit operator PIN or OS authentication can help prevent guests from changing settings or leaving the event flow.
  • Do not set auto return too short. Guests need enough time to scan and save the QR result.
  • For long lines, keep editing time short. For relaxed events, allow more time for text, frames, and stamps.
07

How do I send photos to guests?

The easiest event delivery method is QR. After the result is ready, guests scan the QR with their own phone camera, open the photo, and save it.

  1. Before the event, confirm in the admin screen that QR delivery is available.
  2. After a test shoot, scan the QR with another phone and confirm that the photo opens.
  3. On site, make sure each guest opens and saves the photo before moving to the next guest.
08

What is QR code delivery access?

QR delivery access is the entitlement needed to send event-mode results through QR codes. In the app, it may appear as an event-mode product, subscription benefit, or QR download credit.

  • QR delivery also needs internet access and server delivery status to be ready.
  • If access or credits are missing, QR delivery may be limited. Check the shop or admin area before the event.
  • Product composition can change by app version and store policy, so use the purchase screen as the final source of truth.
09

Can I print photos?

If the event-mode setup supports printer delivery, you can operate photo printing. Printing requires more than an app setting: the on-site printer must be ready.

  • Before the event, check printer power, connection, paper direction, margins, and ink or cartridge status.
  • Print one test result and confirm that the frame is not cut off.
  • If printer setup is risky, QR saving is usually the more stable event flow.
10

Can I request a custom frame for an event?

For custom frames or event-specific designs, contact us by email. Include the event date, purpose, desired text, logo or color direction, cut layout, and whether you need QR delivery or printing.

11

How are the subscription plans different?

Plans differ by which benefits they unlock. In the app, products may be separated by unlimited shooting, unlimited color frames, ad skip, event mode, or QR delivery benefits.

  • Unlimited shooting plans reduce the burden of photobooth film for users who shoot often.
  • Unlimited color frame plans are for users who want more frame color and design options.
  • Bundle or premium products may combine multiple benefits.
  • Monthly and yearly subscriptions may renew automatically. One-time purchases do not auto-renew. Always check the purchase screen for final price and duration.
12

How do I get photobooth film?

Photobooth film is the in-app currency used when saving finished results. You can check your remaining film on the home screen or in the shop.

  • Film may be available through ad rewards.
  • The shop may offer film through points or cash purchases.
  • Unlimited shooting subscriptions or related memberships may change how film is used.
13

What are achievements and points?

Achievements are goals you complete while using the app. Points are in-app rewards you can earn through achievements, attendance, and ad rewards. Points can be used for point-purchasable items such as frame packs or film.

  • If the Achievements button shows a reward, open it and claim the reward.
  • Your current points can be checked from the home screen or shop.
14

How do I earn more points?

The most direct way is to check attendance rewards, ad rewards, and achievement rewards regularly.

  1. Check whether today’s attendance reward is available from the home or achievements screen.
  2. If ad rewards are available, follow the prompt to earn points or film.
  3. Open the achievements screen and claim rewards for completed goals.
  4. Do not manually change device time. Suspicious time changes may lock point earning.
15

What are secret achievements?

Secret achievements are hidden goals whose conditions are not shown before completion. They can unlock when you use different parts of the app, such as shooting, saving, editing, the shop, or event flows.

  • Before unlock, they may appear as secret achievements. After completion, the name and reward condition may be revealed.
  • Some secret achievements may be connected to specific shooting styles or repeated usage patterns.
