01

What is EDSN Frame?

It is an app for taking photo booth pictures with your phone. Choose a cut count and a frame, shoot, and the result screen opens. There you adjust the frame color, bottom caption, stamps, and filters before saving it as a photo or a video.

You can also skip shooting and import photos or videos from your gallery. Saved results go into your device's Photos app and stay there even if you later delete your account.

02

Which frames can I shoot with?

Before shooting, pick either a regular frame or a special frame. Regular frames come in 1, 2, 4, 6, and 9 cuts, combining portrait or landscape with narrow or wide layouts. If this is your first time, a vertical 4-cut is a safe start.

There are three special frames: Color Hunt, Reel Story, and Instant Photo. Each shoots differently, so the cut counts you can pick vary by frame. Instant Photo asks for a paper orientation instead of a cut count.

Turn on automatic orientation in Settings and the app picks the direction that suits your frame. Wide frames work better with the phone laid down, tall frames with it upright, and the capture area stays larger that way.

03

What frame designs are available?

The Frame tab on the result screen offers solid, gradient, and graphic designs. Solid frames stay clean, gradients blend color smoothly, and graphic frames carry a clear theme.

Graphic frames work with the narrow vertical 4-cut frame and the wide B layout, and the shop's graphic frame packs are bought with points. The unlimited color frame membership covers the paid solid and gradient frames, so it is separate from graphic frame packs.

04

How do I use filters or correction tools?

Once you finish shooting or importing, the result screen has a Filter tab and an Adjust tab. The default filters are ready to use. The photo adjustment tools, which go from brightness and color into grain and lighting, and the special-effect filter packs are unlocked in the shop with points or cash.

Filters and adjustments can be applied to one cut at a time or to every cut at once. The same screen lets you change the caption font, size, position, and alignment, and add stamps.

The special-effect filter packs bundle five distinctive filters each, like copier, toy camera, and glitch, and they are not included in the unlimited color frame plan.

05

Can I use it for group events?

Yes. Event mode is for weddings, festivals, stores, and pop-ups where several people take turns on the same device. The operator sets the frame and flow from the admin home, and guests just follow the screen. Results go out by QR or print.

Event mode and printing are free. You only spend QR credits when you send photos by QR.

Prepare before the event

  • A device with the app installed, plus a charger, power bank, and stand or tripod
  • For QR delivery: stable Wi-Fi or a hotspot, a linked Apple or Google account, and remaining QR credits
  • For printing: a printer, paper, and ink. Run printer discovery to confirm it is ready to print right now
  • Photobooth film. Event results consume film on save just like a normal shot, and a session cannot start without it

Before the first guest arrives, have a staff member run through the whole flow once as if they were a guest.

06

What can I set in event mode?

The setup wizard on the admin home defines the entire guest-facing flow: admin PIN, frame design and width, cut count, countdown, which camera to use, the caption font, size, position and alignment, how much result editing to allow, the delivery method, and auto return. The frame and cut count you choose stay fixed during the session.

The admin PIN is four digits. Changing settings or returning to the admin home mid-session requires the PIN or device authentication, so guests cannot wander into your setup.

Auto return is set between 30 and 120 seconds, defaulting to 45. Leave enough time for guests to scan the QR and save the photo. If you allow result editing, the edit window runs from 5 to 60 seconds. Keep it short when the line is long and longer when there is room.

The caption appears small under the result, and leaving it empty hides it. A session cannot start without at least one ready delivery path, so check the readiness indicators on the admin home to see what is blocking it.

07

How do I send photos to guests?

QR is the simplest way. Once the result is ready, guests scan the QR with their own phone camera, open the photo, and save it. The QR link stays valid for 24 hours.

Check QR readiness on the admin home before the event. QR needs a linked Apple or Google account, an internet connection, and remaining credits. It is worth running a test shot and scanning the QR with another phone to confirm the photo actually opens.

On site, wait until each guest has saved the photo before moving on to the next one.

08

What is QR code delivery access?

These are the credits deducted one at a time when you send a photo by QR code. Linking an Apple or Google account gives you five for free, and you can buy packs of 20, 100, 500, or 1,500 in the shop. No subscription is required.

QR codes work in personal shooting too, not just event mode, and both draw from the same account balance. The credits do not expire.

With QR delivery enabled and no credits left, a session cannot start at all, so buy with some headroom for the size of your event. The balance is checked on the server, so an internet connection is required too.

09

Can I print photos?

Yes, though you need a separate photo printer.

Normally you tap the share button on the export screen and choose Print. The share button in your device's Photos app works the same way. In event mode, register a printer in the admin settings and turn on auto print, and each finished session flows into printing. iPhone and iPad send directly to the saved printer; Android opens the system print screen for the operator to confirm.

Pick the printer through discovery or type its ID directly. A saved ID alone is not enough: printer discovery has to confirm it is ready to print before a session can start. If it is not, check power, paper, and network, then search again. You can fill one sheet, place two per sheet, or let the app decide based on your paper.

10

Can I request a custom frame for an event?

Yes, just email us. Include the event date and purpose, the text you want, logo and color direction, cut layout, and whether you need QR delivery or printing. That makes it much faster to work out.

Send it to studioyona.official@gmail.com.

11

How are the subscription plans different?

They differ in what they unlock. Unlimited shooting covers photobooth film, and unlimited color frames covers the paid solid and gradient frames for the length of the plan. A combined plan is available monthly and yearly.

If you shoot often, unlimited shooting fits. If you want the full range of frame colors, take unlimited color frames. If you need both, the bundle costs less than buying them separately. And if you would rather not track a subscription, a one-time purchase opens both permanently. It is not a subscription, so it does not auto-renew.

Ad Skip is also a one-time purchase. It lets you collect ad rewards without watching the ad. Event mode is not a subscription at all; you only buy the QR credits you need for delivery.

Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Cancel from App Store or Google Play subscription management. Deleting your account in the app does not cancel a subscription.

12

How do I get photobooth film?

Photobooth film is the in-app currency spent one sheet at a time when you save a result as a photo or video. Shooting or importing without saving costs nothing, and re-saving the same result without reshooting does not deduct another sheet.

You get three sheets when you first install the app. After that, daily check-in gives one sheet and the ad reward on the home screen gives up to two more per day. Check-in film accumulates until you use it. If you own Ad Skip, the same button gives you the reward without the ad.

You can also buy film in the shop. A single sheet is points-only, while the 5 and 10+1 packs take points or cash. Cash purchases require a linked Apple or Google account. With an unlimited shooting membership, you do not have to think about film at all.

13

What are achievements and points?

Achievements are goals you complete as you use the app, and points are the in-app currency you build up from achievements, check-ins, and ad rewards. Points buy the shop items marked as point-purchasable, like frame packs and photobooth film.

Claim achievement and check-in rewards from the Achievements menu at the top right of the home screen. The button shows when something is waiting. Your current balance and the history of what you earned and spent are on the home screen and in the shop.

Memberships and Ad Skip are cash-only products and cannot be bought with points.

14

How do I earn more points?

Claiming your check-in, ad, and achievement rewards every day is the fastest route. Saving results also earns save-completion points, up to three times a day.

The check-in reward is once a day from the Achievements menu and comes with points plus one sheet of photobooth film. It resets at 4 AM local time. On the same screen, watching an ad through to the end pays out points; if the ad is interrupted or fails to load, it may not. Do not forget to claim the rewards for achievements you have already completed.

There is a referral reward too. When a friend registers your invite code and links an Apple or Google account, both of you receive the reward. One referral reward per device.

Leave your device clock alone. Manual time changes can lock point earning.

15

What are secret achievements?

These are achievements whose conditions are hidden. Some unlock naturally as you keep shooting and saving, and others take something fairly unusual.

While locked, the condition stays hidden; once you complete it, the name and condition are revealed. Rather than hunting for them, just keep shooting and saving and they open one by one.
