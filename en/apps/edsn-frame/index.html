01

What is EDSN Frame?

It is an app for taking photo booth pictures with your phone or tablet. Shoot a variety of N-cut photos and videos, and deliver them by QR or print. It works for everyday memories at home and just as well at events or in stores. Get a custom photo booth for far less than renting or buying one.

02

Which frames can I shoot with?

Combine 1, 2, 4, 6, or 9 cuts with portrait or landscape and narrow or wide layouts.

03

What frame designs are available?

A range of solid, gradient, and graphic frames is available. Six solid colors come free, and additional frames can be purchased with points you earn by using the app. The custom frame feature lets you build a frame in your own design or add a promotional logo.

04

How do I use filters or correction tools?

Use the filter and adjustment tools on the result screen to edit your results. Filters and adjustments can be applied to one cut at a time or to every cut at once. The same screen lets you change the caption font, size, position, and alignment, and add stamps. Try a fun filter and record the memory your own way.

05

Can I use it for group events?

With Event/Store Mode, you can run it like a real photo booth at weddings, festivals, stores, pop-ups, and more. The operator sets the frame and flow from the admin home, and guests just follow the screen. Results go out by QR or print.

Event/Store Mode and printing are free. Only film usage and QR photo delivery carry a separate cost.

Prepare like this

  • A device with the app installed, plus a charger, power bank, and stand or tripod
  • For QR delivery: stable Wi-Fi or a hotspot, a linked Apple or Google account, and photobooth film plus QR credits topped up
  • For printing: prepare a printer and connect it to the app in advance
  • Before the first guest arrives, we recommend a staff member run through the whole flow once as if they were a guest.
06

What can I set in event mode?

You can adjust the frame design, frame width, cut count, countdown, which camera to use, the caption font, size, position and alignment, how much result editing to allow, the delivery method, and the auto return time. The frame and cut count you choose stay fixed during the session.

For security, you can set a four-digit admin PIN. Changing settings or returning to the admin home mid-session requires the PIN or device authentication, so guests cannot change your setup at will.

Auto return after a shoot can be set between 30 and 120 seconds. Leave enough time for guests to scan the QR and save the photo. If you allow result editing, the edit window runs from 5 to 60 seconds. Keep it short when the line is long and longer when there is room.

Photos taken in event mode are not stored on the device; they are delivered by QR, print, or both. A session cannot start without at least one ready delivery path, so check the readiness indicators on the admin home to see what is blocking it.

07

How do I send photos to guests?

QR is the simplest way. Once the result is ready, guests scan the QR with their phone camera, open the photo, and save it. The QR link stays valid for 24 hours and allows up to three downloads.

Check QR readiness on the admin home before the event. QR needs a linked Apple or Google account, an internet connection, and remaining credits. It is worth running a test shot and scanning the QR with another phone to confirm the photo actually opens.

On site, wait until each guest has saved the photo before moving on. Once a session is closed, the photo cannot be brought back.

08

What is QR code delivery access?

These are the credits deducted one at a time when you send a photo by QR code. Linking an Apple or Google account gives you five for free, and you can buy packs of 20, 100, 500, or 1,500 in the shop. No subscription is required.

QR codes work in personal shooting too, not just event mode. With QR delivery enabled and no credits left, a session cannot start at all, so buy with some headroom for the size of your event. The balance is checked on the server, so an internet connection is required too.

09

Can I print photos?

Printing requires a separate photo printer.

For personal shooting, tap the share button on the export screen and choose Print. The share button in your device's Photos app works the same way. In event mode, register a printer in the admin settings and turn on auto print, and each finished session flows into printing. iPhone and iPad send directly to the saved printer; on Android, the system print screen may ask the operator to confirm.

Pick the printer through discovery or type its ID directly. After connecting, confirm it is ready to print right now. If it is not, check power, paper, and network, then search again. You can fill one sheet, place two per sheet, or let the app decide based on your paper. For narrow frames, we recommend the two-per-sheet layout.

10

Can I request a custom frame for an event?

Yes, just email us. Include the event date and purpose, the text you want, logo and color direction, cut layout, and whether you need QR delivery or printing. That makes it much faster to work out.

Send it to studioyona.official@gmail.com.

11

How are the subscription plans different?

Unlimited shooting covers photobooth film, and unlimited color frames covers the paid solid and gradient frames for the length of the plan. A combined plan is available monthly and yearly.

If you shoot often, unlimited shooting fits. If you want the full range of frame colors, take unlimited color frames. If you need both, the bundle costs less than buying them separately. And if you would rather not track a subscription, a one-time purchase opens both permanently. It is not a subscription, so it does not auto-renew.

Ad Skip is also a one-time purchase. It lets you collect ad rewards without watching the ad. Event/Store Mode is not a subscription at all; you only buy the QR credits you need for delivery.

Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Cancel from App Store or Google Play subscription management. Deleting your account in the app does not cancel a subscription.

12

How do I get photobooth film?

Photobooth film is the in-app currency spent one sheet at a time when you save a result. Shooting or importing without saving costs nothing, and re-saving the same result without reshooting does not deduct another sheet.

You get three sheets when you first install the app. After that, daily check-in gives one sheet and the ad reward on the home screen gives up to two more per day. Check-in film accumulates. If you own Ad Skip, the same button gives you the reward without the ad.

You can also buy film in the shop. A single sheet is points-only, while the 5 and 10+1 packs take points or cash. Cash purchases require a linked Apple or Google account. With an unlimited shooting membership, film is unlimited for the duration.

13

What are achievements and points?

Achievements are goals you complete as you use the app, and points are the in-app currency granted through achievements, check-ins, and ad rewards. Points buy the point-purchasable shop items like frame packs and photobooth film.

Claim achievement and check-in rewards from the Achievements menu at the top right of the home screen. The button turns red when a reward is waiting. Your current balance and the history of what you earned and spent are on the home screen and in the shop.

14

How do I earn more points?

Claiming your check-in, ad, and achievement rewards every day is the best route. Saving results also earns save-completion points, up to three times a day.

The check-in reward is once a day from the Achievements menu and comes with points plus one sheet of photobooth film. It resets at 4 AM local time. On the same screen, watching an ad through to the end pays out points. With Ad Skip access, you receive the reward instantly without watching the ad. Do not forget to claim the rewards for achievements you have already completed.

There is a referral reward too. When a friend registers your invite code and links an Apple or Google account, both of you receive the reward. One referral reward per device. Note that manual device-clock changes can lock point earning.

15

What are secret achievements?

These are achievements whose conditions are hidden. Some unlock naturally as you keep shooting and saving, and others take something fairly unusual.

While locked, the condition stays hidden; once you complete it, the name and condition are revealed. Rather than hunting for them, just keep shooting and saving and they open one by one. Find the hidden conditions and collect the points.
