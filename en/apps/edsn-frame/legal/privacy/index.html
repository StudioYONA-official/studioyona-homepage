Additional Notes

  • During ordinary personal shooting and saving, original photos and videos taken through the Service are stored on the user's device or in the user's photo library and are not uploaded to the Company's servers. However, when a linked user directly uses the QR photo delivery feature in personal shooting or Event/Store Mode, the resulting photo image is temporarily uploaded to server storage (Seoul region, Republic of Korea) to issue the download link. Photos taken in Event/Store Mode are not separately saved on the shooting device in principle.
  • Users who operate Event/Store Mode at weddings, events, stores, or pop-ups (event hosts or store operators) are responsible for informing photographed guests of the shooting and QR delivery flow on site and confirming any required portrait or privacy consent.
  • Linking an Apple or Google account is optional, and users may start the core shooting flow in guest mode.
  • Apple DeviceCheck or Google Play Integrity verification information may be sent to Apple or Google to verify and prevent repeated fraudulent claiming of free/reward grants. The Company does not store raw device tokens on its server. The Company stores only the minimum records needed for abuse prevention, such as grant status, grant key, and processing time.
  • The Company does not collect unique identifiers (such as resident registration numbers, passport numbers, or driver's license numbers) or sensitive information (such as health or biometric information).

4. Procedures and Methods for Destruction of Personal Information

The Company destroys the relevant information without delay when the retention period expires or the purpose of processing has been achieved.

  1. Destruction procedures
    • Information whose retention period has expired or whose purpose has been achieved is destroyed without delay unless there is a separate reason for retention.
    • Where separate retention is required by law, the information is kept separately for the applicable period and then destroyed.
  2. Destruction methods
    • Electronic files are deleted using technical methods that make recovery or reproduction difficult.
    • Printed materials and paper documents are shredded or incinerated.

Image files temporarily uploaded for QR photo delivery are disabled upon expiration of the 24-hour validity window or reaching the 3-download limit, and are deleted in a manner that makes recovery difficult under automated or scheduled cleanup procedures.

Hash records used for fraud and abuse prevention do not store original Apple/Google account identifiers, email addresses, device names, or advertising identifiers, and are deleted when the retention period expires.

Operational database backups used for service stability and incident recovery are irreversibly deleted when the configured retention period (up to 14 days) ends and are not retained long-term.

8. Rights and Obligations of Data Subjects and Legal Representatives, and Methods of Exercising Them

Users may exercise the following rights with respect to the Company at any time.

  1. Request access to personal information
  2. Request correction or deletion of personal information
  3. Request suspension of the processing of personal information
  4. Request withdrawal of consent
  5. Request account deletion
  6. Change advertising/tracking consent settings

These rights may be exercised through in-app settings or the contact channel below.

  • In-app features: Account management (account deletion) and legal notices in Settings, plus permission/tracking/advertising settings in iOS or Android system settings
  • Inquiry email: studioyona.official@gmail.com

How to request deletion of your account and related data

Users who link an Apple or Google account may review the deletion impact disclosures and submit an account deletion request directly through the account withdrawal menu under Settings > Account Management inside the app (applicable across Apple App Store and Google Play builds).

If the user cannot access the app or the in-app deletion request cannot be completed, the user may request account deletion by emailing studioyona.official@gmail.com. The Company may request only the minimum information necessary to verify and identify the account.

When the deletion request is completed, the Company deletes or de-identifies account information directly managed by the Company, internal user ID, account-based progress, point/reward status, shop ownership, purchase-restoration linkage information, and account-related information needed for customer support handling.

However, records required for legal compliance, payment settlement, refund or dispute response, security, and abuse prevention may be separately retained for the retention period stated in this Policy or for the period required by applicable law. App Store or Google Play payment records may be retained separately by Apple or Google under their respective platform policies and applicable laws.

Account-linking features are available only to users aged 14 or older, and the Company does not collect personal information from children under 14. If the Company becomes aware that personal information of a child under 14 has been collected, it will promptly terminate the account and delete the relevant data, and a legal representative may request deletion through the contact channel below.

9-3. Methods of Refusal

  1. Refusing iOS tracking
    • Path: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
    • You may disable Allow Apps to Request to Track.
  2. Limiting Apple advertising personalization
    • Path: Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising
    • You may disable personalized ads.
  3. Limiting Android Advertising ID
    • Path: Settings > Privacy > Ads or the advertising/privacy settings provided by the device manufacturer
    • You may delete the advertising ID or limit personalized ads.
  4. Changing settings within the app
    • If the Service provides an advertising/consent-related settings screen, you may change your selected state there.

Even if a user refuses, the core shooting features of the Service may continue to be available, but ad-based rewards or some personalization features may be limited.
