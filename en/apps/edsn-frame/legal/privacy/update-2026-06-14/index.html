EDSN Frame Privacy Policy Update Notice

Effective date of change: 2026.06.14.

1. Updated Document

  • Document: EDSN Frame Privacy Policy
  • Effective date of change: 2026.06.14.
  • Previous update notice: May 12, 2026 backup and recovery operations notice

2. Key Changes

  • We updated the scope of EDSN Frame to cover both the iOS and Android apps.
  • We reflected Android launch-related processing items, including Google Play purchases/restoration, purchase tokens, Play Integrity, Android Advertising ID, and Google UMP consent status.
  • We added clearer account deletion guidance, including the in-app deletion path, email fallback request method, and the types of data that are deleted or separately retained.
  • We clarified that Apple App Store and Google Play payment records may be retained separately by each platform under their policies and applicable laws.

3. Comparison Table

Category Before After
Scope Described primarily as the iOS app Described as applying to both the iOS and Android apps
Account deletion Included account deletion as a data subject right Specified the in-app deletion path, email fallback request, deleted/de-identified items, and items that may be separately retained
Purchases/restoration Focused on App Store transaction identifiers Added App Store or Google Play transaction identifiers and Google Play purchase tokens
Abuse prevention Focused on Apple DeviceCheck verification Reflects Apple DeviceCheck or Google Play Integrity verification depending on platform
Ads/consent Focused on IDFA and iOS tracking settings Added IDFA or Android Advertising ID, Google UMP consent status, and the Android Advertising ID limitation path
International transfer/entrustment Focused on Google sign-in and ad serving Added Google Play purchases/restoration, Play Integrity, and AdMob/UMP purposes
