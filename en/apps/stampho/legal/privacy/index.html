4. Third-Party Disclosure

The Company does not sell users' personal information and does not provide it to third parties without user consent. However, information may be processed according to the user's choice or platform operating policies in the following cases.

  1. When the user directly selects an external app or service, such as a messenger, social network, or cloud service, through the iOS share sheet and shares a file
  2. When Apple processes information according to its own policies during App Store purchases, refunds, downloads, subscriptions, and receipt handling
  3. When there is a lawful request from a relevant authority under applicable law

5. Processing Outsourcing

The Service's core capture, editing, and local record features are not operated through ongoing outsourcing of personal information to external servers controlled by the Company.

7. Destruction of Personal Information

When the processing purpose is achieved or the retention period expires, the Company destroys personal information without delay.

  • When an in-app record is deleted, related local records and app-managed internal media files are deleted.
  • When the app is deleted, data in the app storage area may be removed according to operating system policy.
  • Support emails are deleted after support is completed except for the minimum scope necessary.
  • The Company cannot delete files shared by the user to external apps or services; the user must follow the deletion method of the relevant service.

8. Rights of Data Subjects and Legal Representatives and How to Exercise Them

Users may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, withdrawal of consent, and complaint handling from the Company. Rights requests may be submitted to studioyona.official@gmail.com, and the Company will process them in accordance with applicable law after verifying the requester.

Users may directly take the following actions inside the app.

  1. Delete timeline records
  2. Delete only the photo or delete the entire record
  3. Edit stamp title, content, category, and style
  4. Change app settings
  5. Remove local data by deleting the app

The Company does not provide the Service for children under 14. If the Company becomes aware that it has processed personal information of a child under 14, it will delete the information without delay or review necessary restrictions.

9. Security Measures

The Company applies the following measures to protect personal information.

  1. Captured media, stamp information, and timeline records are basically stored in the app storage area on the user's device.
  2. The Company does not use personalized advertising, advertising tracking, or third-party advertising SDKs.
  3. Unless the user directly initiates sharing, captured results and stamp data are not transmitted externally by default.
  4. App permissions are requested only to the extent necessary to provide features such as camera, microphone, and photo library addition.
  5. If subscription features are enabled, payment method information is processed by Apple's payment system, and the Company does not directly store payment method information.

10. Automatic Collection Devices, Behavioral Information, and Personalized Ads

The Company currently does not use cookies, advertising identifier-based tracking, personalized advertising, or behavioral information-based marketing. If such features are introduced in the future, the Company will provide prior notice and obtain required consent in accordance with applicable law.

11. Automated Decisions

The Company does not operate fully automated decisions that have legal effects or similarly significant impacts on users as a core feature of the Service.
