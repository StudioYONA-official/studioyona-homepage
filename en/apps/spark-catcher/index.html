How Spark Catcher works

Capture without leaving your window, and pin active boards on top

Hunting for a notes window in the middle of coding, writing, or a call breaks your focus. On Mac, double-press `Control` for a note or `Option` for a task over any full-screen app—and return to your exact window and cursor position the moment you close it.

  • Modifier ×2`Control×2` for Quick Note, `Option×2` for Quick Task, or hold to reopen your last entry
  • 1-key scrapsClip selected text (`⌃⌥⌘C`), screen regions with on-device OCR (`⌃⌥⌘S`), or Safari articles
  • Free pinned boardsOpen Command Palette (`Control+Option×2`) and press `Space` to float notes or task boards on top
How it works

Jot it down in place, keep it visible until it is done

Start typing immediately without choosing a folder or template first.

  1. Capture via modifier keys or widgets
  2. One-line tasks & floating boards
  3. Search notes, tasks & OCR scraps
01

Open instant capture from the keyboard or 4-action widget

Double-press `Control` or `Option` on Mac—and type `/` to insert a custom template—or tap the 4-button Home Screen widget (Note, Task, Voice Note, Voice Task) on iPhone and iPad.

02

Start a task in one line and clear it from a pinned board

Write the title first and add due dates, reminders, priority, or groups only when needed. Pin a task board over your workspace so completed items disappear as soon as you check them off.

03

Search text inside screen captures alongside notes and links

Korean and English text extracted from `⌃⌥⌘S` screen captures (via on-device OCR), clean Safari reader clippings, notes, and tasks are all searchable in a single list.

Key features

Free shortcuts & pinned boards, Pro for sync & integrations

Unlimited notes and tasks, Mac global shortcuts, the Command Palette, always-on-top pinned boards, and all five themes are free forever. Try Pro for 14 days with no card required and no automatic billing.
