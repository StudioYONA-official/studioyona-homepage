Spark Catcher Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026

Studio YONA (the “Company”) respects the privacy of people who use the Spark Catcher apps for iPhone, iPad, and Mac (the “Service”) and publishes this policy under applicable privacy law.

The Company does not directly collect personal data during ordinary app use. The Service can be used without account registration and currently does not use advertising, user tracking, analytics SDKs, or a Company-operated server for storing user entries. The Company receives the data in Section 2 only when a user sends a customer-support email.

2. Personal data collected directly by the Company

Collection eventDataPurposeRetention
Sending a customer-support emailSender email address, inquiry content, and user attachmentsHandling the inquiry and investigating errorsOne year from receipt
Attaching a diagnostic fileApp, operating-system, and device information, sync status, and error informationError investigation and customer supportOne year from receipt

A diagnostic file does not include the content of User Entries or account, payment, or authentication information. Email and diagnostics are sent to the Company only after the user confirms sending.

3. Device and Apple data not collected by the Company

  • User Entries, including notes, tasks, and app settings, are processed on the user's device by default.
  • When the user enables iCloud or Apple Reminders integration, the relevant information is processed by the Apple service selected by the user.
  • Processing related to voice input, webpage saving, search, and suggestions occurs on the device when initiated by the user. The Company does not collect that information or the processing results or use them to train artificial intelligence.

4. Retention

The Company retains support emails, inquiry content, and attachments for one year from receipt and deletes them without delay upon expiration of that period. Where a data subject requests deletion, the Company deletes the relevant personal data without delay unless retention is required by applicable law. If a procedure concerning a legal obligation, dispute, or exercise of rights remains pending when the retention period expires, only the minimum necessary data is stored separately until that procedure concludes and is thereafter deleted without delay.

5. Deletion

When the purpose is complete or the retention period expires, electronic files are deleted using methods intended to prevent recovery. The Company does not possess data remaining on a device, in private iCloud storage, or in Reminders; users must manage that data in the app and relevant Apple service. Data shared with another app must be deleted under that service's process.

6. Third-party disclosure

The Company does not sell personal data or user entries or disclose them for advertising. Data is sent to another service only when the user explicitly chooses external sharing or email, or where disclosure is required by a lawful request.

7. External processing

ProviderDataPurpose and methodRetention and choice
Google LLC (Gmail)Sender email address, inquiry content, user attachments, and optional diagnostic fileEmail transmission, storage, and customer support when the user sends emailGoogle states that information may be processed on servers around the world. Data received by the Company is retained under Section 4, while Google's processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Choosing not to send email does not limit the app's core features.

When the user saves a webpage, the selected website may process network information necessary for the page request under its own policy. Apple features selected by the user are governed by Apple's policies.

8. Rights of data subjects and legal representatives

A data subject may access, correct, and delete entries in the app and disable synchronization. Items synchronized with Apple Reminders may also be managed in the Apple Reminders app. Requests to access, correct, delete, restrict processing of, or withdraw consent regarding support data held by the Company, and related complaints, may be submitted to studioyona.official@gmail.com. The Company will verify the requester's identity and process the request in accordance with applicable law. A legal representative may exercise these rights on behalf of a data subject to the extent prescribed by applicable law.

9. Automatic collection and children's data

The Service does not use advertising identifiers, personalized advertising, user tracking, or analytics SDKs and does not automatically transmit diagnostics to the Company. It also does not provide account-registration or personal-data-collection features directed to children under 14.

10. Security measures

  1. User entries are processed by default on the device or through an Apple service selected by the user.
  2. iCloud synchronization and Apple Reminders access occur only when enabled by the user and granted the required permission.
  3. Diagnostics are limited to necessary technical information.
  4. Access to customer-support information is limited to what is needed to handle the inquiry.
