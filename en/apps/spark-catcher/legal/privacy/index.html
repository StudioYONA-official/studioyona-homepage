Spark Catcher Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Studio YONA (the “Company”) respects the privacy of people who use the Spark Catcher apps for iPhone, iPad, and Mac (the “Service”) and publishes this policy under applicable privacy law.

The Company does not directly collect personal data during ordinary app use. The Service has no account registration, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK, or Company-operated data server. The Company receives support data only when a user chooses to send a feedback email.

Feedback and diagnostic transmission are optional and occur only after the data subject confirms sending the email. The diagnostic file does not contain note or task content or titles, tags, group names, Apple Account information, reminder identifiers, email addresses, URLs, or authentication information. A temporary file created on the device for attachment is deleted when attachment-data preparation is complete or when the share sheet closes; an email and attachment that have been sent are subject to the retention period stated above.

3. Device and Apple data not collected by the Company

  • Notes, tasks, task notes, tags, groups, completion status, priority, due dates, alerts, and app settings are processed on the device by default and are not transmitted to the Company.
  • When iCloud sync is enabled, entries and groups are stored in the user's private CloudKit database. The Company cannot view these private records in the developer console.
  • When Reminders integration is enabled, the Service synchronizes in both directions with the Apple Reminders list authorized by the user.
  • The Service does not automatically read from or write to Apple Notes. It sends selected content to the system share sheet only when the user chooses Share.
  • On-device diagnostics retain no more than the latest 120 events and are never sent automatically.

This data is processed by the user's device or a user-selected Apple service to provide app features, but it is not personal data directly collected by the Company.

4. Retention

The Company retains support emails, inquiry content, and attachments for one year from receipt and deletes them without delay upon expiration of that period. Where a data subject requests deletion, the Company deletes the relevant personal data without delay unless retention is required by applicable law. If a procedure concerning a legal obligation, dispute, or exercise of rights remains pending when the retention period expires, only the minimum necessary data is stored separately until that procedure concludes and is thereafter deleted without delay.

5. Deletion

When the purpose is complete or the retention period expires, electronic files are deleted using methods intended to prevent recovery. The Company does not possess data remaining on a device, in private iCloud storage, or in Reminders; users must manage that data in the app and relevant Apple service. Data shared with another app must be deleted under that service's process.

6. Third-party disclosure

The Company does not sell personal data or user entries or disclose them for advertising. Data is sent to another service only when the user explicitly chooses external sharing or email, or where disclosure is required by a lawful request.

8. Rights of data subjects and legal representatives

A data subject may access, correct, and delete entries in the app and disable synchronization. Items synchronized with Apple Reminders may also be managed in the Apple Reminders app. Requests to access, correct, delete, restrict processing of, or withdraw consent regarding support data held by the Company, and related complaints, may be submitted to studioyona.official@gmail.com. The Company will verify the requester's identity and process the request in accordance with applicable law. A legal representative may exercise these rights on behalf of a data subject to the extent prescribed by applicable law.

9. Automatic collection and children's data

The Service and this public page do not use advertising identifiers, personalized advertising, user tracking, analytics SDKs, or web cookies. The latest 120 diagnostic events are retained only on the device and are not transmitted automatically. The Service does not provide account-registration or personal-data-collection features directed to children under 14.

10. Security measures

  1. Entries are processed by default on the device and in the user's private Apple storage.
  2. Sync and Reminders access occur only when enabled by the user.
  3. Diagnostics are limited to necessary technical data and exclude or redact content, identifiers, email addresses, URLs, and authentication information.
  4. Access to support data is limited to what is necessary to resolve the request.
