The Company does not directly collect personal data onto Company servers during ordinary app use. The Company never directly collects or stores credit card numbers, bank account numbers, Apple Account passwords, or other payment credentials. Emails and diagnostics are transmitted to the Company only when explicitly sent by the user.

3. Device and Apple Platform Data Not Collected by the Company

  • User Entries—including notes, tasks, scraps, voice notes, and settings—are processed locally on the user's device by default.
  • Pro Entitlement purchases (auto-renewable subscriptions and non-consumable one-time unlocks), subscription management, and purchase restoration are processed on the device and through Apple App Store (StoreKit).
  • When a Pro user enables private iCloud (CloudKit) or Apple Reminders integration, data is processed within the user's personal Apple Account containers without being sent to Company servers.
  • Voice dictation (STT), webpage saving, text extraction, search, and on-device suggestions run locally on the device when initiated by the user; the Company never collects them or uses them to train artificial intelligence models.

4. Retention Period

The Company retains customer support emails, inquiry contents, and attachments for 3 years after inquiry resolution in accordance with statutory consumer complaint and dispute record standards under Korean e-commerce law, then deletes them without delay (or separately retains statutory contract/payment records for 5 years where applicable). If a legal obligation, dispute, or rights request remains pending upon expiration, only the minimum necessary data is stored separately until that procedure concludes and is promptly deleted thereafter.

5. Destruction Procedures and Methods

When the processing purpose is complete or the retention period expires, electronic files are irreversibly deleted using technical methods and any paper records are shredded or incinerated. Where applicable law requires continued retention, the relevant personal data is stored separately from other data before deletion. Data stored on a device, in private iCloud, or in Apple Reminders is controlled and deleted by the user through the app or Apple settings, and externally shared data is governed by the recipient service's policies.

6. Third-Party Disclosure

The Company does not sell personal data or User Entries or disclose them for advertising. Data is transmitted externally only when the user explicitly selects sharing, when Apple processes App Store billing or user-enabled sync, or where required by a lawful request under applicable law.

8. Rights of Data Subjects and Legal Representatives

Users (and legal representatives) may access, edit, and delete entries directly in the app and disable sync, and may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, or withdrawal of consent regarding support data held by the Company via studioyona.official@gmail.com. The Company verifies authority and responds within the statutory period.

9. Automatic Collection, Automated Decisions, and Children's Data

The Service uses no advertising identifiers, personalized advertising, tracking/analytics SDKs, or automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects on users. It does not direct account registration or personal data collection primarily to children under 14, and any personal information of a child under 14 submitted without legal representative consent is promptly deleted upon discovery.

10. Security Measures and Privacy Officer

  1. User Entries are processed locally on the device or within Apple services enabled by the user.
  2. iCloud sync and Apple Reminders access run only when enabled and authorized by the user.
  3. Diagnostic files exclude entry contents and payment credentials, and support mailboxes are protected with least-privilege access and two-factor authentication.
