This is an English translation of the Korean document. If the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. General

Studio YONA (the "Company") values the personal information of users of the EverScene iPhone and Mac apps (the "Service") and has prepared this Privacy Policy to comply with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea.

2. Personal Information Processed and Purposes

CategoryPurposeItems
On-device processingVideo cleanup, clip saving, transcription, checking the free usage scopeVideos and metadata in the photo library allowed by the user, video files and their metadata (file name, location, size, recording details) in folders the user added on the Mac, folder access information, decision records, hearts, transcriptions, settings
Pro purchaseConfirming and restoring purchasesProduct identifier, purchase status

Exercising rights and legal retentionExercising rights, handling complaints and disputes, retaining records required by lawContact details, request and handling details, minimum materials to verify identity or authority, transaction materials submitted by the user

Photos, videos, files in folders, decision records and transcriptions are processed only on the device and are not collected on the Company's servers. The Mac app does not read files outside the folders the user added. The app changes the photo library and files in folders (deleting, saving clips) only when the user performs an action, and speech recognition takes place on the device. Sharing and exporting original files go directly through the method and location chosen by the user, and the Company does not receive shared materials.

Pro payments are processed by Apple, and the Company does not collect payment method information. Inquiries and attachments are optional and never sent automatically. The Company does not ask for real names, sensitive information, unique identifiers, card numbers or passwords.

Support related to the contract is processed under Article 15(1)4 of the Personal Information Protection Act, and general inquiries and suggestions within the minimum scope necessary for the legitimate interests under Article 15(1)6. Exercising rights and legally required records follow Article 15(1)2 and Articles 35 to 38 of the same Act and Article 6 of the Electronic Commerce Act.

3. Processing and Retention Periods

The Company processes and retains personal information until its purpose is achieved or for the retention period set by applicable laws.

CategoryRetention period
Decision records, transcriptions and settings on the deviceUntil removed with the app's delete function or until the app is deleted (on the Mac, until the app's data is also deleted)
Folder list and folder access information (Mac)Until the folder is removed from the list in the app or the app's data is deleted
Temporary files for clip saving and sharingUntil the task is completed or canceled
Inquiry, suggestion, diagnostic and rights-request materialsUntil the final reply or the request is handled
Records of consumer complaints and dispute handling subject to legal retention3 years under Article 6 of the Electronic Commerce Act and Article 6 of its Enforcement Decree
Records on contracts or withdrawal of purchases5 years under the same laws

Records required by law are kept separately, limited to the necessary materials.

4. Destruction Procedure and Method

The Company destroys personal information without delay once the retention period has passed or the purpose has been achieved.

CategoryProcedure and method
ProcedureDestroyed without delay when the purpose is achieved or the period ends; materials subject to legal retention are kept separately and destroyed when that period ends.
Electronic filesPermanently deleted from the mailboxes, trash and other storage locations managed by the Company.
Printed materialsShredded or incinerated.

On-device data can be erased with the app's delete function or by deleting the app. On the Mac, the app's data may remain after the app is moved to the Trash; in that case delete the app's data for that user account (~/Library/Containers/kr.co.studioyona.everscene). Clips saved to the photo library or folders and shared or exported materials must be managed where they are stored.

5. Provision to Third Parties

In principle, the Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties.

However, where the user has given separate prior consent, or where the law has special provisions or it is required through lawful procedures, it is handled in accordance with the applicable laws. The Company does not provide personal information for advertising or sale.

When a user chooses and uses a sharing method, the terms and policies of that provider apply.

8. Rights and Obligations of Data Subjects and Legal Guardians, and How to Exercise Them

Users may exercise the following rights against the Company at any time.

RightHow to exercise
Access, correction or deletion, suspension of processing, withdrawal of consentRequest through the contact in Section 11.
Changing photo library accessChange in iOS Settings or in macOS System Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

The Company handles requests in accordance with the applicable laws after verifying the user or their representative. On-device data that the Company does not keep must be managed on the device.

Where the law requires consent for information of children under 14, the Company confirms the consent of the legal guardian, and if it learns that information was processed without the required consent, it takes necessary measures such as stopping processing and deleting it.

9. Automatic Collection, Behavioral Information and How to Refuse

The app contains no cookies, advertising identifiers, or advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs, and inquiry materials are not used for personalized advertising or profiling. Videos and usage records are not sent to the Company automatically or used for AI training.

10. Security Measures

The Company takes the following measures to keep personal information secure.

CategoryMeasures
AdministrativeMinimizing access rights, keeping legally required records separately, deleting unnecessary materials
TechnicalStoring usage records on the device, minimizing photo and folder access permissions (on the Mac, the app sandbox gives access only to folders the user chose), deleting temporary files

In case of an incident, the Company carries out notification and reporting under the applicable laws and takes measures to minimize damage.
