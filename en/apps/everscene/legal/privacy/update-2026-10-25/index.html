EverScene Privacy Policy Update Notice

Effective date of change: 2026.10.25.

1. Document updated

  • Document: EverScene Privacy Policy
  • Effective date of change: 2026.10.25. (previous version effective 2026.10.15.)

2. Main changes

  • The policy now applies to both the iPhone app and the Mac app.
  • Video files and their metadata in folders and external drives the user adds in the Mac app are processed only on the device, and files outside those folders are not read.
  • Retention on the Mac, how to delete the app's data, and how to change Photos and folder access were added.
  • What the Company collects, its processors and overseas transfers are unchanged. Photos, videos and decision records are still not collected on the Company's servers.

3. Comparison

Item Before After
Scope iOS app, Pro purchase, customer support iPhone and Mac apps, Pro purchase, customer support
On-device processing items Videos and metadata in the photo library, decision records, transcriptions, settings Adds video files and their metadata (file name, location, size, recording details) in folders the user added on the Mac, folder access information and hearts
What the app changes Changes the photo library only when the user performs an action Changes the photo library and files in folders (deleting, saving clips) only when the user performs an action; does not read files outside the folders the user added
Retention On-device data until removed with the app's delete function or the app is deleted On the Mac, until the app's data is also deleted; folder list and access information until the folder is removed from the list or the app's data is deleted
Destruction The app's delete function or deleting the app Adds how to delete app data that may remain on the Mac after the app is deleted
User rights Photo library access changed in iOS Settings Adds changing Photos access in macOS System Settings and removing a folder from the list in the app's sidebar
Security measures Minimizing photo access permissions Minimizing photo and folder access permissions (on the Mac, the app sandbox gives access only to folders the user chose)
