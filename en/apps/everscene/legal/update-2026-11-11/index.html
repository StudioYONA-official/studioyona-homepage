EverScene Terms of Use and Privacy Policy Update Notice

Effective date of change: 2026.11.11.

1. Documents updated

  • Documents: EverScene Terms of Use, EverScene Privacy Policy

2. Main changes

  • Both documents now also apply to the Android app.
  • Terms of Use: Pro for the Android app is purchased on Google Play and can be restored with the same Google Account, and prices, payment, withdrawal and refunds follow Google Play's procedures.
