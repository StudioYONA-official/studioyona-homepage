How EverScene works

Save the 10-second highlight, delete the 5-minute original

Photos are easy to delete from a single thumbnail, but videos pile up because you cannot tell what happens inside without playing them through. EverScene shows every scene and spoken line without turning on audio, and lets you save just the highlight as a new clip so you can delete the multi-gigabyte original.

  • 10× Silent SkimmingCheck videos without sound using 10× speed, 5-second thumbnail grids, and speech subtitles
  • Save Clip & Clear OriginalPreview the estimated clip size, save the section as a new file, and queue the original for deletion
  • 2-Step Safe DeletionReview candidates before sending them to Recently Deleted or Trash, where they stay recoverable
Key features

From your phone's camera roll to Mac folders and external SSDs

No sign-up or cloud upload required—every video review and speech transcript runs directly on your device.

  • Sort while watching with swipes or keyboard arrow keys

    Swipe up for Delete Candidate, down for Keep, or left for Later. On Mac, sort with single arrow keys or two-finger trackpad swipes, and press ⌘Z to undo multiple steps in a row.

  • Full-video thumbnail grids and on-device speech subtitles

    Open Overview to expand a long video into 5-second interval thumbnails and tap any frame to jump right there. Turn on Korean or English speech subtitles to read conversations without turning on sound.

  • Start with your largest videos, or filter by date and album

    Sort largest-first when you need gigabytes back immediately, or pick a specific month, custom date range, or Photos album to clean up at your own pace.

  • Mac folders, external SSDs & SD cards, and 4-video comparison

    On Mac, clean up videos across your Photos library, Finder folders, external SSDs, and SD cards in one window. Your sorting progress persists even after unplugging and reconnecting a drive, and you can play up to four nearby videos side by side to pick the best take.

  • Free to start, one-time Pro with no subscription

    Sort your first 30 videos for free, with 5 free videos granted every day after that. EverScene Pro is a one-time purchase—never a recurring subscription—and a single purchase unlocks Pro on both iPhone and Mac.

How it works

Three-step video cleanup

  1. Skim & sort fast
  2. Scan all scenes at once
  3. Save clip & clear original
01

10× silent playback & speech subtitles

Scrub the bottom frame strip or watch at up to 10× speed with speech subtitles, then swipe or press an arrow key to mark Delete Candidate, Keep, or Later.

02

5-second thumbnail grid (Overview)

Expand any long video into timestamped thumbnails to see every scene at a glance, tap a frame to play from that point, and mark Keep or Delete Candidate right from the grid.

03

Save Clip & Clean Up Original

Drag the trim handles to select the section worth keeping, check the estimated file size, and tap Save Clip & Clean Up Original to save a standalone clip and move the heavy original to Delete Candidates.
