Veruma is a local iPhone camera app used without account registration. Capture, live preview, frame captions, gallery, settings, authorized Photos browsing, and local diagnostics operate exclusively on the user's device. The Service operates no proprietary accounts, server uploads, cloud sync servers, targeted advertising, analytics SDKs, or automated decision-making.

4. Third-Party Provision

The Company does not sell personal information or disclose it to third parties without consent, except where the user initiates sharing via the iOS share sheet (governed by the recipient's policy), separately consents, or where required by a lawful request from a public authority under applicable law.

5. External Platforms and International Processing

6. Destruction Procedures and Methods

When the processing purpose is fulfilled or the retention period expires, personal information is promptly destroyed. Electronic files are irreversibly deleted using technical methods, and any paper records are shredded or incinerated (records required by law are stored separately until expiration).

7. Rights of Data Subjects and Legal Representatives

Users (and legal representatives) may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, or withdrawal of consent regarding support records held by the Company via studioyona.official@gmail.com. Device permissions (Camera, Microphone, Photos) and local media can be managed directly in iOS Settings, the Photos app, and in-app delete controls.

The Service does not direct account registration or personal data collection primarily to children under 14, and any personal information of a child under 14 submitted without legal representative consent is promptly deleted upon discovery.

8. Cookies, Tracking, and Automated Decisions

Veruma uses no cookies, advertising identifiers (IDFA), personalized advertising SDKs, or behavioral analytics tools, makes no automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects on users, and never uses user photos or videos to train AI models.

9. Security Measures and Privacy Officer

  • Separate add-only vs. read-only Photos permission prompts and strictly on-device media processing
  • Bounded rotation and path redaction for local diagnostics, exported only upon explicit user action

11. EEA / EU Privacy Notice

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or European Union (EU), the Company processes personal data in support communications on the legal bases of contract performance (paid entitlement guidance and customer support responses) and compliance with legal obligations (GDPR Article 6), relying on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses where international transfers occur. EEA/EU users may exercise rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent via studioyona.official@gmail.com and may lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority.
