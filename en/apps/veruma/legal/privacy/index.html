Veruma Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 19, 2026

Studio YONA (the "Company", "we", "us") values the privacy of users of the Veruma iOS app (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we process personal information in connection with Veruma.

Veruma is an iPhone camera app that can be used without account registration. Core capture, live preview, frame caption, gallery, settings, shutter customization, and app-icon features operate on the user's device. The Service currently does not provide account registration, login, server upload, cloud sync, advertising, analytics SDKs, personalized advertising, or AI-based automated decisions.

4. Third-Party Provision

The Company does not sell users' personal information and does not provide it to third parties without consent. However, information may be processed according to the user's choice or platform policies in the following cases:

  1. The user directly shares captured images through iOS sharing features or another app.
  2. Apple processes information under its own policies for App Store purchase, refund, download, subscription, or receipt handling.
  3. A lawful request is made by an authorized public authority under applicable law.

5. Processing Entrustment

The Service's core camera and saving features are not operated by constantly entrusting personal information to Company-controlled external servers. Support email handling may use the infrastructure of the email service provider.

If we introduce analytics, crash reporting, cloud storage, advertising, or a separate payment verification server, we will update this Privacy Policy with the actual processor, task, retention period, and international transfer details where required.

6. International Transfer

The Company currently does not collect personal information to Company servers or transfer it internationally for Veruma app features. App Store purchase, refund, subscription processing, and iCloud Photos sync may be processed according to Apple's terms and privacy policy. This is not an international transfer directly performed by the Company.

7. Destruction

When the processing purpose is fulfilled or the retention period expires, the Company destroys personal information without delay. Electronic files are deleted in a manner designed to make recovery difficult, and paper records, if any, are shredded or destroyed by an equivalent method.

Images, in-app gallery items, and settings stored on the user's device can be deleted by the user through app features, the iOS Photos app, iOS Settings, or app deletion. Deleting the app may not automatically delete images already saved to the Photos app.

8. User Rights

Users may request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, withdrawal of consent, data portability, and complaint handling under applicable law by contacting `studioyona.official@gmail.com`. The Company will process requests after verifying identity and according to applicable law.

Because Veruma does not store membership accounts or captured images on Company servers, Company-managed information that can be accessed, corrected, or deleted is limited to information voluntarily provided to the Company, such as support inquiries. Users may manage app permissions and local photos through the following paths:

  • Camera permission: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera > Veruma
  • Photos add permission: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Veruma
  • Saved photos: delete the relevant images in the iOS Photos app
  • In-app gallery: use Veruma's gallery delete feature

The Service is not directed to children under 14. If the Company learns that it has processed personal information of a child under 14, the legal representative may exercise rights through the contact above.

9. Cookies, Behavioral Information, and Advertising Identifiers

Veruma currently does not use cookies, web beacons, advertising identifiers (IDFA), personalized advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking tools, or analytics SDKs. If these features are introduced later, we will update this Privacy Policy with the collector, items, purpose, retention period, and opt-out method, and obtain consent where required.

10. Automated Decisions

Veruma does not currently make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on users. The app does not use users' photos, camera frames, or settings to train AI models.

11. Security Measures

The Company applies the following measures to protect personal information:

  1. The app requests add-only Photos permission and does not request full Photos library read access.
  2. Camera preview frames and captured images are not uploaded to Company servers.
  3. The app does not directly collect account passwords, payment card information, or advertising identifiers.
  4. Users may allow or deny app permissions through iOS permission controls.
  5. Access to personal information included in support inquiries is limited to the support purpose.
