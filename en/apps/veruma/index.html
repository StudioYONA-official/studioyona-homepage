Key features

We refined the parts you use every time

We paid attention to how the shutter responds, what stays recorded under the photo, and whether your settings survive a relaunch.

  • Photos as records

    Frame captions and shot numbers carry over, so your library reads like a gallery.

  • Room to customize

    A manual mode for serious photographers, plus a range of custom controls.

  • Veruma Pro

    Includes every shutter painting, 4K preview capture, Control Center access, and app icon changes.

Where it fits

Reach for it when

  • On a trip, when you want the sky and the air of that day exactly as you saw them.
