How Veruma works

Preview-frame capture with no post-shutter color shifts

Veruma records both still photos and HEVC videos straight from the live filtered viewfinder stream without running post-shutter auto-enhancement. Your chosen aspect ratio (4:3, 3:2, 1:1, 16:9), front-camera mirror setting, and filter grain or halation are saved just as they appeared on screen.

  • True to previewSaves the exact colors, crop (4:3, 3:2, 1:1, 16:9), and selfie mirror state shown on screen
  • Painting & film tonesFresco, Gold Leaf, Fuji-inspired, retro film, and vintage digicam looks with live Strength, Mist, and Soft controls
  • 64 art shuttersShoot with 64 classic paintings by Van Gogh, Monet, Klimt, and Hokusai in Sequential, Random, or Fixed mode
Shooting flow

Direct controls right below the viewfinder

Swipe through filters and drag exposure controls directly below the viewfinder without opening nested settings menus.

  1. Preview-frame capture
  2. 64 painting shutters
  3. Live painting & film filters
  4. Direct to device Photos
01

Live exposure, white balance & aspect ratio

Changes to exposure (EV), white balance (K), zoom, and aspect ratio appear live in the viewfinder and save to your photo or video without post-shutter shadow boosting.

02

64 classic painting shutter buttons

From Van Gogh's The Starry Night and Hokusai's Great Wave to Klimt's The Kiss and Monet's Water Lilies, choose which paintings appear on your shutter—rotating in order, changing at random, or staying fixed on a single favorite.

03

Painting, film & retro filters with live sliders

Explore collections ranging from Fresco and Gold Leaf painting tones to Fuji-inspired color curves, retro film grain, and early-smartphone (3GS-style) textures. Pin up to 8 favorite filters to your personal rail to switch with a horizontal swipe, and fine-tune filter Strength, Mist, and Soft halation live.

04

Local Photos library storage with no sign-up

Captures go directly to your device's system Photos library with no sign-up, cloud uploads, ad networks, or analytics tracking.

Key features

Built for both quick one-handed snaps and manual control

Every shooting control sits cleanly below the portrait viewfinder so your frame stays unobstructed.

  • Direct-drag adjustments & Manual (M) mode

    Press and drag directly on the white balance, aspect ratio, EV, or zoom buttons to change values with tactile haptics—no pop-up dial required. Switch to M mode for direct control over shutter speed, ISO, and manual focus with live focus peaking.

  • Film date stamp, external mic option & free Control Center launch

    Imprint a classic film-style date stamp onto still photos, opt into connected external microphones for video recording, and launch Veruma straight from iOS Control Center or the Lock Screen for free.

  • Veruma Pro (flexible subscription or one-time purchase)

    Shoot watermark-free with core filters and painting shutters for free. Upgrade to Veruma Pro to unlock the full catalog of painting, film, and retro filters, all 64 art shutters, 4K preview capture, and alternate app icons—available via monthly/yearly subscription or a single lifetime purchase.

Good for

When to use Veruma

  • Sunsets & backlit scenes — Keep natural evening sky gradients and gentle silhouettes without aggressive HDR brightening flattening the mood.

  • Exhibitions, cafés & dim interiors — Preserve deep shadows and warm indoor lighting as they actually look in the room.

  • Portraits & mirror selfies — Capture natural skin textures and your exact mirror framing with subtle Mist or Soft halation and zero post-shutter oversharpening.

  • Everyday walks & travel logs — Open straight from Control Center and swipe across your personal 8-filter rail to shoot matching photos and videos on the spot.
