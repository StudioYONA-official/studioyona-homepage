Family & Pet Camera

Peekaboo Cam

Draws children and pets to look right at the camera lens instead of staring down at the middle of the screen. Play up to five recorded family voices and show animated cues next to the front lens—while saving clean photos and videos with zero overlays.

  • 5 recorded family voices (plays during video) · 0.5x–3x optical zoom & auto macro

Viewfinder Camera

Veruma

Built for moments when you love the live preview, only for the Photos app to brighten shadows and oversharpen faces after capture. Dial in Fresco, Gold Leaf, or film tones in the viewfinder and save that exact color and crop without post-shutter color shifts.

  • Saves the exact color, tone, and aspect ratio shown in the viewfinder
  • 64 master-painting shutters · Manual mode (M) · Film date stamp

  • 6–24 layer real-time mandala drawing across 10m–4h timers
  • Lock Screen Live Activity · Optional Screen Time app shielding
